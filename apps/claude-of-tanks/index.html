    About Claude of Tanks

    Shells travel, armor has real geometry, modules break, buildings fall and wrecks stay. Modes include Standard Battle, Capture the Flag, Frontline Assault with a six-operation campaign, Endless Horde co-op, Gun Game and more, plus a practice range. Up to eight phones can join one couch battle from a QR code on the big screen.

    You can also watch bot battles, ride along in any tank, inspect any vehicle's armor and internals in the Tank Gallery, or stage scenes and capture video in Scene Studio. It works with mouse and keyboard, controllers or touch, in English and Simplified Chinese. All vehicle models and battlefields are the maker's own.
