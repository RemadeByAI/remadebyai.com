    About Sakura Fantasy

    You sit in a reed-roofed river boat while a boatman sculls you downstream, then the season turns and the journey begins again. Watch, or take the camera and stop to look around. It uses three.js and WebGL 2 with no game engine, and was featured in Ruan Yifeng's weekly and the Codrops Creative Hub. From the maker of Neon Zenith.
