    PrintStash

    Alternative to Manyfold

    A self-hosted library for your 3D models that keeps each model's files, G-code revisions, slicer settings and print results together, and can send jobs to your printers.

    About PrintStash

    Upload models and archives or index a folder or NAS in place, then sort them into collections with tags, filters and saved views. It previews meshes and G-code layers, compares slicer settings between revisions, lets you mark the known-good one, and records print outcomes, time, cost and filament (with optional Spoolman). It reads STL, 3MF, OBJ, STEP, G-code and more, but does not slice models itself.

    It runs as one Docker container on SQLite, with PostgreSQL, S3 storage and OIDC sign-in as options. Moonraker/Klipper is the main printer integration; Bambu LAN, PrusaLink, OctoPrint and Elegoo Centauri Carbon support is in beta.
