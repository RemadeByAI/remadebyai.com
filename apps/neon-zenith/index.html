    About Neon Zenith

    On the street the car hovers a hand's width off the road; in the air it holds altitude through five lanes of sky traffic up to a spire 1,418 metres high. There are four time trials with medals, 31 hidden data shards, a city map and a radio. Rain, lightning and a score that builds with speed are all made at runtime.

    It runs on three.js with WebGPU and a WebGL2 fallback, and works with keyboard, gamepad or touch, with a lighter mode for phones.

    How it was built with AI

    The README says it was built over a weekend in October 2026 with Claude Code running Claude Opus 5.5: the maker set the direction and reviewed every pass, and the model wrote the code, the shaders and the Blender pipelines.
