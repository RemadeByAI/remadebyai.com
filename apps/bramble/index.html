    About Bramble

    Bramble runs as a browser extension for Firefox and Chromium browsers, a desktop app for Mac and Linux with a quick-access panel, sync hub and scheduled backups, and iPhone and Android apps with system autofill, biometric unlock and passkeys. All of them share one Rust encryption core, and devices sync directly with each other, so your vault is never stored on a server.
