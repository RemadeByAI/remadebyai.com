    About SuperCmd

    SuperCmd opens from one shortcut and runs existing Raycast extensions without changes, and can import a Raycast backup. It also has hold-to-speak dictation, read-aloud for selected text, AI chat with the provider of your choice, clipboard history, snippet expansion, quick links, notes, a drawing canvas, file search and 24 window-tiling commands.

    The open-source v1 on GitHub is free under the MIT licence. Its website sells a newer v2 for a one-time $19.99 (covers 3 Macs, 7-day free trial).

    What people say

    SuperCmd launched on Product Hunt and was written up by the German Apple site ifun.de. Some users said it covered everything they used in Raycast and liked that it was fast, free and open source. Critics said it copies Raycast's design too closely, and some readers disliked that it was built largely with AI. Early users also reported a bug where search results could not be clicked with the mouse, which the developer said was fixed. Coverage is fairly thin and mostly from around launch.
