    About ArtCraft Launcher

    Each ArtCraft app ships its own builds for every platform. The launcher lists the whole suite (PhotoCraft, LightCraft, FilmCraft, DesignCraft, VectorCraft, PdfCraft, EffectCraft, AniCraft, WordCraft, DeckCraft, GridCraft, CADCraft, SoundCraft, FigCraft, TextureCraft, MaterialCraft and more), shows which ones you have, picks the right installer for your system, checks it against the release's SHA-256 checksums and tells you when an update is out.

    It is a small native app that can sit in the menu bar or system tray, and the Windows installer needs no admin rights. Its README marks it early alpha.
