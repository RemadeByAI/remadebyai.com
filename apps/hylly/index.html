    About Hylly

    Hylly (Finnish for shelf) lists everything you own on Humble Bundle, StoryBundle, DriveThruRPG and itch.io and downloads it into one folder, checking every file against the shop's checksum so a sign-in page is never kept as a book. It finds covers and details, and you can add your own files too.

    It then serves the library to your browser, with a reader, and to reading apps over OPDS, so phones, tablets and e-ink readers can browse it like a bookshop and download just the books they want. It can alert your phone through ntfy and verify that files are still intact. It is a small Python command-line toolbox installed with uv; it comes with notes and skills so an AI assistant can help set it up.
