    About Flowplan

    Flowplan keeps documents, databases with table, board, calendar, timeline and other views, whiteboards, handwritten notebooks, a daily journal, and projects with tickets and sprints in one page tree. Teams edit together live with cursors, comments, mentions and suggested changes, and can share pages with guests by link or publish them on the web. It imports from Notion, AppFlowy, Obsidian, Jira and Trello and exports to Markdown, CSV and ZIP.

    It runs as a single Docker container with SQLite, with PostgreSQL as an option for larger teams, or you can use the hosted version at app.flowplan.org, which runs in Germany. It installs as an offline-capable web app, with desktop apps for macOS, Windows and Linux, an iPhone and iPad app, and an Android app downloaded from the site. AI features are optional and off by default, and can use a local model through Ollama or your own key for a cloud service.
