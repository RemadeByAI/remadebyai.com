    About Meerkat CRM

    Meerkat CRM is a digital address book that reminds you of birthdays and helps you remember things like dietary habits. Group contacts into circles, record relationships and job history, keep a timeline of notes and activities, and see your contacts as a network graph. A built-in CardDAV server syncs both ways with your phone's contacts.

    It runs on your own server, and there is a live demo.

    What people say

    Coverage is thin and comes mainly from a launch thread in the Lemmy self-hosted community, where reactions were warm. Commenters liked the idea, the dark mode, the ready-made demo and the developer's open disclosure about using AI. Questions focused on data export, CalDAV support for birthdays and reminders, notification options, and how it compares with Monica or a plain contacts app. A blog write-up repeats the README's points about data ownership and a lightweight stack.
