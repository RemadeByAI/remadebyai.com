    About Second Tail

    Sal can double jump and climb, Ben can dash and ram, and the way home needs both of them. The demo covers rooftops, a mine, an aqueduct, a market and more, with several boss fights, a race in the rain and puzzles about freeing prisoners. It supports English and Ukrainian, controllers, rebindable keys and a reduced-motion option.

    It is an early demo still in development, made in Unity. It plays in the browser on a computer or a phone (with on-screen controls) and is also a Windows download on itch.io. The first browser load is about 190 MB. The characters and art are made with AI image generation.
