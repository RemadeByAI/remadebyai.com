    About Aurora Courier

    You fly a small courier ship through the northern lights, bringing lanterns to a mountain village before dawn. Each gold ring is a portal: sky, rocks, lights and even the music snap into a new style, from pixel art and woodblock prints to neon wireframes, oil paint and synthwave.

    It is calm but quick, with two modes and four endings, cutscenes and procedural music. It is one HTML file with nothing to download or install.
