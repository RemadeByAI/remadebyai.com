    About PaletteRider

    Each chapter turns a real artwork into a little city you ride through. In Klee's patchwork town you collect fallen suns and bring them to the castle; in Van Gogh's Arles you carry stars back to the glowing café and watch them return to the sky; in Monet's Giverny you gather flowers by the lily pond; and in Hodler's Jungfrau you ride the real Lauterbrunnen valley, built from map and elevation data.

    Get your load home before dawn and land your jumps cleanly, or half of it slips away. The paintings are public-domain reproductions.
