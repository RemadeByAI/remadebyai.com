    About WallpaperMachine

    Wallpaper Engine is Windows-only. WallpaperMachine is an independent Mac app, not affiliated with Wallpaper Engine or Valve, that plays its wallpapers with its own Metal renderer. You can browse the Steam Workshop inside the app without signing in, but downloading Workshop wallpapers needs a Steam account that owns Wallpaper Engine (Steam checks this), and scene wallpapers also need shared resources from a Wallpaper Engine you have bought. The app bundles neither. Without Wallpaper Engine you can still use the bundled video wallpaper, your own videos and pictures, and pixiv illustrations. Compatibility varies by wallpaper.

    Each display can have its own wallpaper, and there are playlists, schedules, audio-reactive wallpapers, desktop widgets such as clocks and calendars, Shortcuts support and battery rules that lower quality or pause playback. It needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later, and installs from a free DMG or with brew install --cask wallpapermachine. A paid Priority Support option exists but unlocks no features.
