    About Game Launcher for macOS

    It finds installed Steam games (on the Mac and inside CrossOver bottles) and Mac apps marked as games, and opens each one the right way: Steam starts the game, native apps open directly, and CrossOver opens the right bottle first. Your library has grid and list views, search, favourites, play status, notes, collections and covers from Steam or your own images.

    It also tracks play time, takes screenshots with a key or controller button, backs up save folders, and can be driven with an Xbox, PlayStation or Switch Pro controller in a full-screen mode. Needs macOS 14 or later; it isn't notarised, so the first launch needs a right-click and Open. Brand new (first release 2026-11-27).
