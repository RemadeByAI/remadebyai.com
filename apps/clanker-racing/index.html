    About Clanker Racing

    Flat-shaded 240-line graphics, uneven terrain, no barriers and limited nitrous: you can drive off the track and explore, and take shortcuts if you dare. The five AI drivers use the same physics as you, brake for bends, take shortcuts, use nitrous on straights and, on harder settings, try to block and bump you. Tracks include a river crossing, a lava bridge, a frozen lake and Tranquility Base with Earthrise.

    It is plain JavaScript and WebGL 1 with no libraries or server, so you can also download it and open index.html. Keyboard, mouse and gamepad work; phones technically work too.
