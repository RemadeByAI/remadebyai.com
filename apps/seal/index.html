    About Seal

    You write one envelope per person: a letter, photos, a voice message, a video, files and secrets like passwords. A few trusted people each hold a piece of the key, added in person. If you don't open the app for a set time (90 days by default), one of them can start a claim, you get daily warnings for weeks, and only then can enough key holders release the envelopes. Each one opens only for the person it was written for, and one tap from you stops the process at any point.

    The envelopes are encrypted on the phone before they're stored in iCloud, and there's no password or email account, just a passkey or a security key. The app is for iPhone and iPad on iOS 18 or later; recipients and key holders can open envelopes in a browser. It's free to install, and the owner pays once to seal envelopes. The maker says the code hasn't been independently audited yet.
