    About StykAnimate

    StykAnimate opens Pivot (.piv, .stk) and Stick Nodes files and can save them back. You pose jointed figures with inverse kinematics for arms and legs, build your own figures from lines, circles, shapes, text and images, and animate either on a simple Pivot-style frame strip or on an advanced timeline with layers, keys and easing. Onion skins, motion paths, camera moves, particle effects and a shared library of figures from other animators are included.

    Exports cover MP4, WebM with transparency, GIF, APNG, image frames and editable project files. It runs on Windows and Linux (installer, portable or AppImage) or in the browser at app.stykanimate.com, which works offline once loaded. Everything runs on your own device; the desktop app sends anonymous usage and crash statistics, which you can turn off in Preferences.
