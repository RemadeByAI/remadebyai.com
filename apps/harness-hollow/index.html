    About Harness Hollow

    Two farmers share one farm and see each other's actions as they happen: plant and harvest crops, raise animals, run workshops with recipe queues, fill orders and quests, decorate, and grow a village across the river. It has 40 levels, day and night, seasons and weather, touch controls with a phone layout, and English and Bulgarian.

    On the free public server you start a farm in one tap and invite your partner with a link; farms nobody visits for 7 days are deleted. You can also run it yourself with Node.js 24 on a home computer or a Raspberry Pi, where an optional helper can use your own Claude or ChatGPT sign-in to turn a typed wish into a game mod.
