    About Tidewater

    Bites depend on the water, the depth and the time of day, across 18 Caribbean species. Keep the line tension in the green band while the fish runs, then log its length and weight. Joe's fish stand buys your catch and Marta's chandlery sells line, reels, rods, fuel, a fish finder and floodlights for night fishing. You can swim the reef and watch a humpback breach.

    It runs directly on WebGPU with its own small rendering engine and needs a recent Chrome, Edge or Safari and a capable graphics card. The first load can take a minute while shaders compile. Progress is saved in the browser.
