    About Postfalcon

    Postfalcon schedules posts for Mastodon, Bluesky, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Telegram, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest and LinkedIn (X too, if you pay for X's API). One composer cross-posts to several channels with per-platform options, a calendar lets you drag posts to new times, posts can repeat daily, weekly or monthly, and an analytics page pulls back views, likes, comments and shares. A REST API, an MCP server for AI assistants and outbound webhooks are included.

    You can self-host it for free with every feature: a one-line installer sets it up with Docker on a Linux server with a domain, using about 100 MB of memory. The hosted version at postfalcon.co is free for Mastodon, Bluesky and Telegram, and paid plans for the other platforms start at $29 a month with a 7-day trial.
