    About Rembrandt

    Rembrandt develops RAW files from over 1,000 cameras and covers the everyday Lightroom work: tone and colour sliders, curves and grading, masks (brush, gradients, AI subject, sky, depth and people), healing and removal, lens corrections, presets and film looks, HDR, panorama and focus-stack merges, AI denoise and super resolution, and a photo library with albums and search. You can type or say edits like "warmer and a bit brighter" and watch the sliders move, using a small language model that runs on your computer.

    Edits are saved as standard XMP sidecars next to your photos, so your originals are never changed and other editors can read the edits. There are installers for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux, or you can run it as a small server on the computer that holds your photos and edit in any browser. AI features can be switched off entirely. The builds are not code-signed yet, and the project warns that its editing engine is still changing between versions.
