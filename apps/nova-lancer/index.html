    About NOVA LANCER

    Mission 01, Azure Coast: fly over a sunset sea full of rock arches, through a winding red canyon with falling pillars, and into a neon fortress city, then fight Mizuchi, a mechanical sea dragon that rises from the water. Charge shots lock on to whole formations, and you can barrel roll, boost, brake and drop smart bombs, while three wingmen and your commander talk over the radio.

    Terrain, sea, sky, ships and the boss are all generated in code, and the music, sound effects and radio voices are synthesized. It works with keyboard, mouse, gamepad or touch, with three difficulty levels. The game text is in Japanese.
