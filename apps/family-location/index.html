    About Family Location

    Positions come from the Home Assistant Companion app on each phone, and everything else is worked out in your own Home Assistant: no accounts, no subscription. Add places by typing an address, then choose who gets told when someone arrives or leaves, and for whom (only the kids at school, say). A trip only ends after five minutes without moving, so traffic lights don't cause false arrivals, and people travelling together don't get alerts about each other.

    It also shows who is still, moving or driving, logs trips with distance and top speed, warns about speeding and low batteries, and has check-in and SOS buttons, with a map card for the dashboard. Install it through HACS as a custom repository. English and Spanish.
