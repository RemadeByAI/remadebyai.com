    About openGym

    Plan a routine for each weekday (or a rotation) from a library of over 5,600 exercises with animated demos and a body map, or start from one of four ready-made plans. During a workout, weights are filled in from last time, a rest timer runs between sets and personal records are spotted as you go. It handles supersets, drop sets, timed and cardio exercises, and works out plate math from the plates your gym has.

    You run it on your own server with one docker compose command; it installs to the home screen, works offline and signs in with passkeys. There is an Android APK, an iPhone and Apple Watch app, and a live demo in the browser. No account on anyone else's server, no subscription, no ads.
