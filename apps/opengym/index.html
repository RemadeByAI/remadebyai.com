    About openGym

    Plan a routine for each weekday (or a rotation) from a library of over 5,600 exercises with animated demos and a body map, or start from one of four ready-made plans. During a workout, weights are filled in from last time, a rest timer runs between sets and personal records are spotted as you go. It handles supersets, drop sets, timed and cardio exercises, and works out plate math from the plates your gym has.

    You run it on your own server with one docker compose command; it installs to the home screen, works offline and signs in with passkeys. There is an Android APK, an iPhone and Apple Watch app, and a live demo in the browser. No account on anyone else's server, no subscription, no ads.

    How it was built with AI

    What people say

    Users are very positive: it averages 4.9 out of 5 on the App Store, and reviewers on AlternativeTo and the self-hosting blog noted.lol praise it as free, ad-free and in your control, with a good exercise library, rest timer and progress tracking. Complaints are minor: the app occasionally closes between exercises, there is no alert when a rest period ends, and running your own server takes upkeep. One reviewer points out it was built heavily with AI.

    People like

    • Free with no ads or subscription
    • Large exercise library, previous weights pre-filled, built-in rest timer and progress charts
    • Self-hosting keeps workout data on your own server

    Common complaints

    • App can close when moving between exercises on iPhone
    • No vibration or sound when a rest period ends
    • Self-hosting means running and updating your own server
    • Built heavily with AI, which some users dislike
