    About KAIJU DOKAN!

    Play as Dokagon and knock down 40% of the city in four minutes. Buildings are made of floor-by-floor blocks, so only the part you hit breaks; take out the base and a tower topples into its neighbours. Keep smashing to build a combo of up to x5, fill your energy for a magma breath, and fight off the tanks and attack helicopters that come after you.

    The graphics, sound and music are all generated in code. Play with mouse and keyboard, a gamepad or touch. The game text is in Japanese.
