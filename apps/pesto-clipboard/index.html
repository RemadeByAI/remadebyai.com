    About Pesto Clipboard

    Pesto Clipboard sits in the menu bar and remembers more than 500 things you have copied, including text, images and files. Press Cmd+Shift+V (or your own shortcut) to open the history, type to filter it, and paste an item with Return or by its number from 1 to 9. Shift+Return pastes without formatting.

    It needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later and installs from a DMG or with Homebrew. History is kept in a local database on your Mac, and copies from password managers such as 1Password and Bitwarden are skipped by default. The app is not sandboxed and does not encrypt its history itself; it relies on FileVault for that.
