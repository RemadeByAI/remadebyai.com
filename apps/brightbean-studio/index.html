    About BrightBean Studio

    BrightBean Studio plans, schedules and publishes posts to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, Google Business Profile and DEV.to (X is not supported). It is built for people running many client accounts: unlimited organisations, workspaces and team members with roles, approval stages with comments, a no-account magic link so clients can approve posts, a unified inbox for comments, mentions, DMs and reviews, per-post analytics, a media library, a Kanban idea board and recurring posting slots on a calendar.

    Every feature is free, with no paid tier. You can use the free hosted version at brightbean.xyz, deploy it with one click to Heroku, Render or Railway, or run it on your own server with Docker Compose (Django and PostgreSQL). Self-hosting means creating your own developer credentials with each social network.
