    About GymCoach

    Log sets quickly (shorthand like 100x8@9 works), with a rest timer, plate calculator, supersets and offline logging as an installable web app. Progress pages chart estimated 1RM and weekly volume per muscle, with a muscle heat map, records and a training calendar. It includes classic programs like 5/3/1, GZCLP and StrongLifts, logs cardio, and imports history from Strong, Hevy, Apple Health, Strava and watch files.

    What sets it apart from other self-hosted trackers is the coach: add an Anthropic, OpenRouter or local model key and it gives weekly reviews, answers questions about your training and generates programs from one sentence. Without a key it is a plain tracker. It runs with Docker on an x86_64 server, and there is a live demo.
