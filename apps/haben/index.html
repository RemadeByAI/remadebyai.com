    About Haben

    Haben covers the path from receipt to tax office: write invoices (including ZUGFeRD and XRechnung e-invoices), collect receipts, match bank payments, and check and send VAT returns. It does EÜR or full balance sheets with E-Bilanz, and is built for the GoBD rules with locked bookings and an audit log.

    You run it on your own Linux server with Docker or Podman. It can import invoices, receipts and contacts from Lexware Office or sevDesk and recalculates past years so you can compare before cancelling. AI features are optional and can use a model on your own server.
