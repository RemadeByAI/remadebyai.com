    About Inkline Town

    You pick the size and richness of a new island and a term of 45, 60 or 90 minutes, then lay roads, house settlers and build production chains: lumber and planks, stone and ore for tools, wheat to flour to bread, wool to cloth, and furniture for the finest homes. Settlers move up to citizens and burghers as their streets improve, while you juggle taxes, trade, winters, fires and the crown's demands. Each term ends with a verdict and a chronicle of how it went, and you can stand again to carry on the same town.

    Everything is drawn in code as line art, with no image files. It runs in a browser on a computer or a phone, saves stay on your device, and you can share a link that opens your town for someone else to play on.
