    About Emberwick

    Emberwick is an original fantasy adventure: explore a 3D world from above, talk to characters, take on quests with companions and follow its plot. It also has a level editor. It runs in a desktop browser.

    Its maker wrote about building it, and about how much better AI-made games got in October 2026, on his blog at simonwillison.net.

    How it was built with AI

    Simon Willison built it in the Claude chat app and wrote that everything in the game, "the graphics, the plot, the characters, the music, the dialog", came from the model (simonwillison.net, 2026-10-28).
