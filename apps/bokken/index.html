    About Bokken

    There are no weapon types: you carve wood with a sharp stone, hone edges, join pieces of different materials and set the grip, and the game works out how the result swings. You steer your arms directly to swing and thrust, break scenery and knock weapons out of enemies' hands while crossing an island past a bandit, a monk and an Oni to face a Master. Fires heal you, and weapons chip and break, so you keep rebuilding them. There are three modes: one-life Realism, a more forgiving Gamified mode with checkpoints, and Endless.

    It is a one-week prototype made with Godot for an AI game jam (Jamference: AI Game Jam Hack 2, October 2026). It plays in the browser with keyboard and mouse, a gamepad or touch (landscape only on phones), and there are downloads for Windows and Linux. The crafting takes some learning; the in-game help (H) explains it.
