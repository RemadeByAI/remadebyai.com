    About FewChat

    Someone comments your keyword on a post or reel and gets your DM with the link in about a second, plus an optional public reply. It also triggers on story replies, story mentions, DM keywords and first messages, and the flow builder lays out buttons, delays, conditions, A/B splits and tags as cards on a canvas.

    It uses Instagram's official API, keeps contacts and conversations in your own database and has no per-contact pricing. Setup takes about 45 minutes, mostly in Meta's developer site, and one command walks you through it. The software is free, but real use needs Cloudflare's Workers Paid plan ($5 a month); optional AI replies are off by default.
