    About Hearth for webOS

    Hearth replaces LG's busy home screen with something quiet: the time, date and weather, your apps and TV inputs along the bottom, and a background that is OLED black, a picture or a slideshow of your own photos. It starts almost instantly and everything is adjustable from the remote.

    It installs as a normal webOS app and works on webOS 3 up to the newest versions. The README explains ways to open it automatically, with or without a rooted TV.
