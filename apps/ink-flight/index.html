    About Ink Flight

    Glide a white paper plane over ink-hatched mountains fading into blue haze, a teal river, a pen-drawn town with terracotta roofs, a bridge, a windmill and a lighthouse on the cliffs, all in ink and light watercolour on old yellowed paper.

    Play with keyboard and mouse. It's also on Wavedash.
