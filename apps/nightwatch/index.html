    About Nightwatch

    Nightwatch sits in the Mac menu bar and checks the forecast every 30 minutes, using Apple Weather or Open-Meteo for cloud plus 7Timer for seeing and transparency. An hour before sunset it sends one alert if there's a long enough clear run in full darkness, along with a second forecast's opinion. It also shows a sky score, a plan for tonight with the best time for each target, the week ahead, events like meteor showers and ISS passes, and darker sites nearby.

    You can tell it how much sky your trees and houses block in each direction, or measure that from a phone photo, and it only suggests targets that clear it. It knows your telescope or smart scope and suggests filters, exposures and frame counts. The Mac download needs macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon, and the app is also free on the App Store for Mac, iPhone and iPad.
