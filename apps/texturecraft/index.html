    About TextureCraft

    TextureCraft paints PBR materials straight onto 3D models in a layer stack with masks, generators and smart materials. Brush strokes flow across UV seams, mesh maps bake on the GPU, and a path-traced Rendering mode previews the result.

    It imports glTF/GLB, FBX, COLLADA, USD, Alembic, OBJ, PLY and STL, and exports textures as PNG, JPEG, TGA, TIFF, EXR, HDR and more at 8, 16 or 32 bits with UDIM tiles. Materials from MaterialCraft can be sent straight to its shelf. Its README (2026-11-01) estimates about 93% of a texture artist's daily workflow works end to end.
