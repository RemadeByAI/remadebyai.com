    About Folio

    Folio opens PDFs in tabs and remembers where you left each one. You can highlight in colours with notes, draw, add text boxes, sign with a drawn signature, print booklets, and read in dark mode. It also opens PDF links straight from the web, turns a web page into a selectable PDF, and converts Word files to PDF when you open them.

    Ask AI is optional: ask about the whole document or a passage and get answers with page links, using your ChatGPT plan, your Claude subscription through Claude Code, an OpenRouter key or a local model. Builds are not code-signed yet, so Windows and macOS warn on first launch.
