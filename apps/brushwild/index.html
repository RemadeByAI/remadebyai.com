    About Brushwild

    Pick one of three classes (the sword-and-shield Warden, the spell-casting Emberwright or the bow-wielding Strider), grow a seven-branch skill tree, chase loot through five rarities and clear dungeons across a procedural world. Every pixel is drawn by shaders to look like pen hatching and pencil on cream paper, with night falling as a darker layer of ink.

    The whole game is a single small HTML file. It plays with mouse and keyboard, a gamepad, or touch controls on a phone held either way.
