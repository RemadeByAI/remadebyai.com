    About Bambu Bar

    Bambu Bar shows percent done and time left in the menu bar, and a click opens a panel with the job, its stage, nozzle, bed and chamber temperatures, AMS spools with grams left and printer errors in plain words. You can pause, resume or stop a print, change its speed, switch the chamber light and watch the printer's camera. It watches several printers at once, adds desktop widgets and sends notifications when a print finishes, fails or pauses.

    It talks to the printers directly over your local network, with no account or cloud, and reads access codes from Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer during setup. Print controls need the printer in LAN Only mode with Developer Mode on. The Mac app needs macOS 26 or later and the Windows app Windows 10 (1903) or 11. The GitHub build is free; a one-time $9.99 supporter licence adds automatic updates.
