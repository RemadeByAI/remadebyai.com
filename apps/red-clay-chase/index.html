    About Red Clay Chase

    The police play roles: one rams your bumper, two box you in, one gets ahead and brake-checks you. There are five wanted levels, and at five stars a timer starts: survive it and the cops give up. Hoods flip up and tear off, and doors swing open and fly off on side hits.

    No game engine or physics library: driving, crashes and collisions are written from scratch on three.js, and every texture, car, tree and sound is made in code. Touch controls work on phones.
