    About OpenTodo

    Type a task the way you say it ("Call Sarah next Wed 3pm #work p1") and OpenTodo sets the date, project and priority. Today shows what is due, you can drag tasks onto a day or week calendar to block out time, and Focus runs a Pomodoro or countdown timer that logs time to the task. Lists can be shared with family or a team, and it works offline.

    You run the server yourself (Docker, a single binary for Windows, Mac or Linux, Unraid, Home Assistant and more) or rent a hosted copy from PikaPods. There is an iPhone and iPad app in the App Store and an Android APK. Everything is free, with no paid tier.
