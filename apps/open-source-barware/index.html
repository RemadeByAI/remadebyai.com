About Open Source Barware

Walk your shelves and say what you see ("Tito's, half. Jameson, full.") and Open Source Barware builds the count sheet, prices every bottle and tells you what's short and what to reorder. Import last week's sales from your POS and it flags bottles that came up short.

It runs on your own Mac or Windows laptop, works without internet or AI (AI is optional, for reading invoice photos), counts in English or Spanish and can run several locations. There is a sample bar to try in the browser. It is free with no paid tier, under GPLv3.

How it was built with AI

The maker says AI is what let people from the bar trade build the tool themselves. The GitHub repo keeps CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md files for AI coding agents, and 56 of its 249 commits (checked 2026-11-22) are co-authored by Claude.
