    About Open Source Barware

    Walk your shelves and say what you see ("Tito's, half. Jameson, full.") and Open Source Barware builds the count sheet, prices every bottle and tells you what's short and what to reorder. Import last week's sales from your POS and it flags bottles that came up short.

    It runs on your own Mac or Windows laptop, works without internet or AI (AI is optional, for reading invoice photos), counts in English or Spanish and can run several locations. There is a sample bar to try in the browser. It is free with no paid tier, under GPLv3.

    How it was built with AI

    The maker says AI is what let people from the bar trade build the tool themselves. The GitHub repo keeps CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md files for AI coding agents, and 56 of its 249 commits (checked 2026-11-22) are co-authored by Claude.

    What people say

    Most of the discussion comes from one Hacker News thread, and it was largely skeptical. Some commenters saw it as a possible free alternative to paid bar inventory services. Others questioned why invoice reading needs a cloud AI, called it a thin layer over a spreadsheet, and said the polished, AI-written website made the project harder to trust. A Product Hunt launch drew only a handful of upvotes and one positive comment.

    People like

    • Could replace paid bar inventory subscriptions
    • Local-first design, with the cloud used only for reading invoice photos
    • Interface looks polished

    Common complaints

    • Uses cloud AI for invoice reading when local OCR could do the job
    • Seen by some as a spreadsheet with an opinionated front end
    • Marketing site felt AI-generated and short on a plain description of what the program does
    • No iPad or mobile app for counting stock behind the bar
