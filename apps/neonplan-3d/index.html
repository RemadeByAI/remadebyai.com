    About NeonPlan 3D

    NeonPlan 3D adds a floor plan editor to Home Assistant: draw floors, rooms, doors, windows and roofs, or import Sweet Home 3D, iPhone LiDAR scans or CAD files. The house then shows live in 3D on a wall tablet or dashboard, with lights in their real colours, moving blinds, cameras, energy flows and single-room cards. Styles range from glowing neon to homely wood floors and an architect's white model.

    It installs through HACS and runs fully locally. The core is free and MIT licensed; a free optional account unlocks fun packs, and paid Pro add-ons cover energy, cars, household and heat pumps. A free community fork, NextFloor, also exists.

    What people say

    Coverage is thin: a launch thread on the Home Assistant forum with a few replies, and a German blog review of its fork NextFloor that also weighs up NeonPlan. Forum users who tried it were impressed by how much of their house they could recreate. The complaints are about placing doors and the project website being mostly in German.

    People like

    • Detailed recreation of a real house
    • Users call it the best 3D view they have seen in Home Assistant
    • Seen as the more mature project next to its fork

    Common complaints

    • Doors often land in the wrong orientation and small objects are hard to move
    • Much of the website and manual is in German
