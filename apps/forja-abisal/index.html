    About Forja Abisal

    A tribute to the first-person shooters of the 1990s: maze-like, vertical levels full of enemies, keys, doors, lifts and secrets, from the lava channels of Fundición Cero to the open pit of Pozos de Ceniza. An automap fills in as you explore.

    All the models, textures, sounds, levels and names are original and generated by code, using Three.js and Rapier physics. The game is in Spanish, and you can also download it and run it locally.
