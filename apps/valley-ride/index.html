    About Valley Ride

    You ride a bay horse up a 2.5 km trail through meadows and pine woods, past four pairs of lanterns, to a campfire on the summit. The sun sinks as you climb, so you arrive at sunset, and the horse slows to a stop by the fire. Morning, noon, sunset and night are a click away; at night the lanterns light the path and fireflies drift over the meadow.

    The horse's gallop was checked frame by frame against Muybridge's 1878 photographs. It needs a WebGPU browser (current Chrome or Edge on a computer), a keyboard and a mouse, and the first visit downloads about 74 MB.
