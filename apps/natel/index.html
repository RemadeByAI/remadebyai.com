    About Natel

    If you keep your address book on a CardDAV server so it syncs to your phones and computers, Natel gives you a clean place to search, fix and organise those contacts in a browser, on a computer or a phone. It does contacts only: no calendars, tasks or mail, and it does not host address books itself.

    It runs as one small Docker container that you point at your server (or without Docker), and you sign in with your CardDAV account. It keeps fields it does not show, warns you instead of overwriting a contact someone changed on another device, and keeps your password in memory only. There is a public demo with made-up contacts that reset on reload.
