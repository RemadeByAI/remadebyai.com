    About Monido

    Once a month you type in a balance for each account, investment, property and loan, and Monido works out what the family owns after debts, in every currency, and how that changed. It shows real growth after inflation, savings and the savings rate per month, how many months the emergency fund would last, and when you will reach each goal.

    It runs as one Docker container (Node and SQLite), small enough for a Raspberry Pi or a NAS, and works on a phone as an installable web app. An optional Claude advisor can weigh a purchase or a loan against your own numbers. There is a live demo with a made-up family, and the app speaks seven languages.
