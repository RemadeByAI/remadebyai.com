    About OpenTV

    OpenTV plays live TV from your own provider's Xtream Codes login or M3U playlist; it does not come with any channels. It has an XMLTV guide that survives restarts, DVR recording to the box, a USB drive or a NAS with series links, catch-up where your provider offers it, reminders, movies and series with resume, favourites, picture-in-picture and a parental PIN.

    It was written from scratch so that it can't vanish the way paid "lifetime" IPTV apps sometimes do: the source is public and anyone can keep it going. Favourites and watch history sync between your own devices with no server in the middle. One APK works on TVs, phones and tablets, and it is built for a remote.
