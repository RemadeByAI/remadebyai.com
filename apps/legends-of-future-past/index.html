    About Legends of Future Past

    Legends of Future Past was one of the first commercial online multiplayer games, a text world (a MUD) set in the Shattered Realms of Andor with eight races, five schools of magic, psionic combat and over two thousand rooms. Computer Gaming World gave it a Special Award for Artistic Excellence in 1993.

    The original engine's source code was lost, but former Game Masters had kept the script files that define every room, item, monster and quest. The new engine runs those original scripts, so the world is the real one. The server is free to play, and you can host your own copy.

    How it was built with AI

    Jon Radoff, who made the original game in 1992, says in the README that he used Claude Code to reconstruct it from the old script files, manuals and captured play sessions, in a weekend of agentic engineering. Checked 2026-11-22: all 120 commits are co-authored by Claude.

    What people say

    The AI-assisted revival got notable attention in Chinese tech and games media, which framed it as a 1990s online game brought back in a weekend with Claude. A 17173 writer who played it said the game beat their expectations and felt immersive despite being text only, though the lack of Chinese support was a hurdle. Former players commenting on the maker's write-up were mostly nostalgic and grateful, with one doubting the 'one weekend' framing.
