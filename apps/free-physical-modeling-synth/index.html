    About Free Physical Modeling Synth

    Every note is calculated sample by sample inside the browser's AudioWorklet: a hammer striking stiff, slightly inharmonic strings for the piano, plucked strings with a body for guitar, koto, harp and shamisen, bowed strings for violin and cello, air jets and reeds for flute, clarinet and trumpet, and resonators for marimba, vibraphone and bells. There are no recordings in it.

    Play with the on-screen keys or the computer keyboard, change brightness, decay and reverb, or turn on a headphone 3D mode that puts the piano about a metre in front of you. Drop a General MIDI file on the page and it is played with all 128 GM sounds and the drum kit mapped to the models. The piano was tuned against Steinway and Yamaha recordings and the drums against an open drum sample set. The page is in Japanese with an English summary.

    How it was built with AI

    The README says the code was written by Claude in Claude Code, with the author playing it by ear many times and Claude tracing each complaint against real piano recordings to fix it. Checked 2026-11-28: 8 of 12 commits carry Claude co-author lines.
