    About Atlantis

    Concentric rings of canals circle a stepped podium with a 105 m crystal rising out of a crystal lotus, spiral towers, glass domes, villas hidden in flowering trees, a harbour, farmland and limestone mountains. Press Enter and fly anywhere; there are no goals, it is a place to explore.

    It is one 95 KB HTML file that works offline and needs WebGPU (Chrome or Edge on a computer). The prompt and the art bible are published in the repo.
