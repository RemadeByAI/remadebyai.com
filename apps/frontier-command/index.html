    About Frontier Command

    An original 3D RTS about economic base building and tactical unit combat. Harvest resources, put up buildings, train units and push the enemy back across maps like the Meridian Riverlands, with bridges, fords and varied ground. Army colours and settings are saved.

    New players can start with Training, eight guided lessons on selecting, moving, harvesting, building, producing, attacking and pulling back. It plays on desktop with keyboard shortcuts, or on a phone or tablet with tap, drag and pinch controls. The maker also publishes the full prompt used to build the game.
