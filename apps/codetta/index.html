    About Codetta

    Codetta runs next to your music and opens in any browser, including your phone. Edit tags for one track or a whole album with a preview before anything is written, match albums on MusicBrainz (with Discogs, Deezer and iTunes as fallbacks), rename and move files with a folder pattern, and point it at a downloads folder so new music is matched and filed. A library health check finds split albums, missing covers and duplicates, and every change can be undone from the activity log.

    It handles FLAC, MP3, M4A, OGG, WAV, AIFF, WMA and more, and tells Navidrome, Jellyfin, Emby, Plex and Lidarr to rescan after changes. It runs as a small Docker container (Intel/AMD or ARM, Raspberry Pi 4 and up), or on a Linux or Mac computer via pipx (Python 3.14) or snap. It is still labelled beta, so keep a backup of your music.
