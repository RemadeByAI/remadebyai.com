    About Felt World

    Everything in Felt World looks knitted, felted or stitched from yarn. Drive a car down the knitted streets of Buttonbury, take off as a felt bird over meadows and knit-row mountains, or go under the Deep Felt Sea in a submarine or as a sea turtle among fish schools, kelp, rays and crabs.

    The whole game is one HTML file with no image or audio files: every texture is drawn on a canvas and every sound is made with Web Audio. It runs in a desktop browser and on a phone held sideways, with touch controls.
