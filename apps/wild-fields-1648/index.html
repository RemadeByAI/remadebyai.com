    About Wild Fields 1648

    Raise a Cossack town on the steppe: farm, fell forests, quarry stone and mine gold, then train pikemen, musketeers, Cossack horse and more to take on AI opponents that build, gather, research and attack on their own.

    The details are what stand out: wheat that grows and ripens, paths that wear into the ground where peasants walk, trees that fall and leave stumps, burnt buildings that leave ruins, and people and horses with proper walking and galloping animation. It uses 20 free photo-scanned models from Poly Haven, and all the sound is synthesized in code. It runs in one desktop browser tab.
