    About HomeRoll

    HomeRoll is an independent client for a self-hosted Immich server, built to feel like Photos on iOS 26: the grid with its zoom levels, the viewer, the editor, memories, search, people and background backup of new shots. The maker uses it every day against a library of about 85,000 photos and videos.

    It isn't on the App Store. You install it with SideStore or AltStore (a free Apple ID works, with a refresh every 7 days), or build it with Xcode. English and German. A macOS sibling, HomeRoll Desktop, shares the same edits. Not affiliated with Apple or Immich.
