    About Wisp Player

    Wisp plays MP3, FLAC, WAV, AAC, Vorbis, Opus, ALAC and AIFF, plus the sound of video files, with sample-exact gapless hand-over, ReplayGain and smooth fades that never click. Its audio thread never waits on the interface, and it reopens the output device on its own if it disappears.

    The library scans folders in the background and offers songs, albums and artists views, accent-insensitive search, a queue and playlists. The look borrows from Apple Music, with an artwork-tinted backdrop and synced lyrics, and there are 35 themes from Nord to Windows 95. A careful tag editor lets you pick the right album match before anything is saved. It is written in Rust with no WebView; only Windows is built so far.
