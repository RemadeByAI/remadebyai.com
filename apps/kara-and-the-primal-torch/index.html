    About Kara and the Primal Torch

    A cult has put out every fire of Kara's tribe except the Primal Torch. You run and jump through the fallen camp, the Ancestral Sands and the Sleeping Volcano, holding fire to let the torch hunt the nearest enemy. Firing drains the flame and shrinks its light, so you fight in the dark if you burn too long. Bonfires act as checkpoints, three guardian bosses wait at the ends of the stages, and secret chambers hide nine captives to free for the true ending.

    There is an Arcade mode with chained kills for score and a Time Attack mode. It plays with keyboard, gamepad or touch (landscape on phones). It was made in Godot for the AI Browser Game Jam 5, and its status on itch.io is On hold.

    How it was built with AI

    The maker says on itch.io: "Almost everything in the game was generated with AI, directed and curated by Egobiian", with "Code, stages and the design document: Claude Code by Anthropic, working as a small team of specialised agents". Graphics came from PixelLab, music from ACE-Step and sound from MOSS-SoundEffect (https://egobiian.itch.io/kara-and-the-primal-torch).

    What people say

    The only feedback comes from players on itch.io, mostly fellow entrants in the AI Browser Game Jam 5, where it placed 21st overall from 7 ratings. Players like the pixel art, the heroine, the 90s feel and the way firing darkens the screen. Criticism covers the simple first boss, the lack of health pickups in early versions and a short minimum jump; the maker has updated the game since several of these comments. Coverage is thin.

    People like

    • Strong pixel art and smooth animation
    • Firing that darkens the world makes the theme clear
    • A convincing 90s arcade feel

    Common complaints

    • The first boss is basic
    • Early versions had no way to recover health
    • The short-press jump is hard on higher walls
