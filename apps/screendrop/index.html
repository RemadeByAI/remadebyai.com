    About Screendrop

    Screendrop lives in the Mac menu bar and captures a display, a window or an area, with a hotkey for each plus one that copies on-screen text with OCR. New captures land in a floating stack where you can copy, pin, annotate or upload them, and a Library window keeps every screenshot and recording in one place. The annotation editor is non-destructive, with arrows, shapes, text, smart redaction, crops, backgrounds and borders you can change later.

    Recordings keep the screen, camera, cursor, clicks, keystrokes and audio as separate tracks, so you can add zooms, a smoother cursor, keystroke captions, camera layouts, cuts, speed changes and on-device captions after the fact. There is also a teleprompter that follows your voice. Sharing is optional and runs on your own Cloudflare account. It needs macOS 26.4 or newer, installs from a DMG or Homebrew, and the maker warns it is under active development with occasional breaking changes.
