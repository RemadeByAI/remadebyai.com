    Minute

    Alternative to Granola, Otter.ai

    A Mac meeting note-taker that works with no internet at all. It notices when a call starts, transcribes it live, then writes a summary, decisions and action items with a local model.

    About Minute

    Minute records your microphone and, if you allow it, the other people on the call, with no bot joining the meeting. It can spot when Zoom, Teams, Slack, FaceTime, Webex, Discord or a browser call starts and offer to record, then stop by itself when the call goes quiet or ends. Whisper writes the transcript as people talk, and when you stop, a local language model writes a summary, the decisions and action items you can tick off. You can ask questions about one meeting, label speakers by voice and have them recognised next time, search every transcript, and save each note to an Obsidian vault as Markdown.

    Everything runs inside the app: there is no account, no analytics and no cloud AI service to plug in, and it only goes online to download models or check for updates. It is a quiet note-taker rather than a live call coach. Notes are plain files on your disk, and audio is deleted after 30 days by default. It needs macOS 11 or later (13 or later to record system audio), at least 8 GB of memory and about 3 GB of disk for the default models. Apple silicon is the tested target; an Intel build exists but is untested.
