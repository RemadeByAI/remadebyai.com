    About Ink Away

    Ink Away adds a pen to KOReader, the free e-book reader that runs on jailbroken Kindles, Kobos and Android. You can draw with many pens (fineliner, fountain, pencil, watercolor and smudge) on up to five layers, keep notebooks on lined, grid or planner pages with handwriting turned into text, and write on any book KOReader opens, with the ink following its words when you change the font size.

    Books you have written on can be exported as PDFs that keep your ink and searchable text. It needs a stylus-capable reader; palm rejection needs KOReader 2026.07 or newer. Install it by copying the folder into KOReader's plugins directory.
