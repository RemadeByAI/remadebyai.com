    About Readfine

    Readfine is a web feed reader in the spirit of Tiny Tiny RSS. Subscribe to RSS and Atom feeds or YouTube channels, scrape sites without a feed using a CSS selector, or save any link to read later. Every new article gets a relevance score against topics you list, without any AI, so the best ones come first, and when several feeds cover the same event they collapse into one row. Filters can label, star, mark read or archive articles using regex and AND/OR conditions. The reader pulls in the full article text, plays YouTube and Vimeo videos in place, and has two- or three-panel layouts, a proper phone layout, dark mode and keyboard shortcuts. OPML import works with Tiny Tiny RSS.

    AI features are optional and use your own key: summaries, a chat about an article, and "Catch me up" digests, also by email on a schedule, with Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini or a local model through an OpenAI-compatible endpoint such as Ollama. You can sign up for free at readfine.app (up to 150 feeds) or host it yourself with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL; the setup script needs Linux, macOS or WSL2.
