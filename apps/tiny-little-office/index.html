    About Tiny Little Office

    There are ten apps: Word Processor, Outliner, Presentation Builder, Spreadsheet, Calculator, Drawing Tool, Diagram Maker, Animator, Calendar and Trip Planner, in 21 languages. Each document is packed into the page's address, so copying the link copies the document and whoever has the link has it. Because of that, documents have to stay small.

    By default nothing is stored anywhere. You can choose to keep documents on your own device, or make a short link that is encrypted with a key the server never sees. AI agents can make documents too, through an llms.txt file, an npm package or a Claude Code skill. Developers can run their own copy; the code needs Node 24 or later.

    What people say

    Tiny Little Office got a lot of attention at launch in October 2026: a Hacker News thread with more than 700 points and 200 comments, plus write-ups in The Register and TechRadar. People like the clever idea of the link being the document, the lack of accounts and how polished it looks, and some single out apps like the Animator and Trip Planner. Critics point out that documents must stay small because they live in the URL, ask why not just use LibreOffice, and doubt how long a quickly built, AI-coded project will last. Early users also hit some bugs, and the site went down under the launch traffic.
