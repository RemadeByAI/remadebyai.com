    About A Game About Capybaras Delivering Food

    Made in about two weeks by a solo iOS developer for Cursor Vibe Jam 2026, where it won and took home $25,000. Multiplayer runs over WebSockets on Cloudflare. It was covered by Wccftech and Mac Observer.

    How it was built with AI

    The maker says the game was entirely vibe-coded, running two or three Claude Code sessions at once, each on a different part of the code (reported by Wccftech and Mac Observer). The 3D city map was finished by hand in an editor.

    What people say

    Players who rated the game on the VibeReview site are very positive, giving it 4.6 out of 5 across 88 reviews, but almost all comments are one or two words, many in Portuguese, praising it as fun and full of detail. Blog round-ups list it among the best AI-built browser games and single out its developer write-up as worth reading. Coverage is thin on actual critique: most press stories focus on the $25,000 Vibe Jam win and the AI-built code rather than on how the game plays.
