    About Minilytics

    Minilytics shows visitors, revenue, conversion rate and revenue per visitor, with charts by hour, day, week or month. It breaks down traffic by channel, referrer, campaign, country and device, tracks pages, outbound clicks, goals and funnels, and links each paying customer to the visits that led there. A live globe shows who is on your site right now.

    Each person deploys their own copy to their own free Cloudflare account, so the data stays with you.
