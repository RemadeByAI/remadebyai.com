    About MX Options

    Logitech makes no Options+ for Linux, so this fills the gap. Every button takes one of about 70 ready actions, a shortcut, a command or a macro. Gestures fire on swipes in four directions or on sixteen drawn shapes, each with its own vibration. The Actions Ring opens a menu of bubbles around the cursor, with dials you turn with the wheel for volume, tabs or zoom.

    It also sets pointer speed, SmartShift and ratchet force, plays game rumble on the mouse, and has Flow: push the pointer off the screen edge and carry on on a second computer, clipboard included. Built and tested on KDE Plasma 6 with an MX Master 4; GNOME, sway, Hyprland and X11 desktops are handled too, and a Windows build can be made from source. Not affiliated with Logitech.
