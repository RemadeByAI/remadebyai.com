    About Reelbird

    Reelbird records a display, a window or an area, with camera, microphone and system audio on separate tracks. With sharing on, it uploads while you record and puts the link on your clipboard when you stop. Later you can open the same take, where it has already planned zooms on your clicks and typing, smoothed the cursor and levelled your voice, then trim, blur sensitive text, add captions or a background and update the video behind the existing link. It can also turn a recording into a step-by-step guide.

    The app itself is free, with no account and no telemetry, and links can point at your own storage bucket such as Cloudflare R2. If you'd rather not set one up, the optional paid Reelbird Pro plan ($5 a month) hosts your links and adds viewer stats. It needs an Apple silicon Mac on macOS 15 or later, and it includes a command-line tool and an MCP server so coding agents can record demos.
