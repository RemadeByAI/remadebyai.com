    About Arena Game

    Matches last five minutes. You earn money over time and from kills, spend it on tanks, helicopters, snipers, rocket launchers and super units that march toward the enemy base, and claim turrets, forward bases and barracks along the way; whoever scores the most points wins. You can queue for a real opponent, practise against Easy, Medium or Hard AI on two maps, watch live games and climb a leaderboard, with a choice of visual themes and touch controls on phones.

    You can play as a guest or sign in with GitHub or BlueSky to keep your stats. The server is written in Go with a Three.js client, and you can build and host your own copy as a single binary.
