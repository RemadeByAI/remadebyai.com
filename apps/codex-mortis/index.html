    About CODEX MORTIS

    $3.99 on Steam, and Steam Deck Verified. It entered early access in March 2026 and reached version 1.0 in September 2026. Its free demo drew 10,500 players, and Steam reviews are Very Positive. Its claim to be the world's first fully AI-made game was covered by PC Gamer, TweakTown and Creative Bloq, and split opinion among players.

    How it was built with AI

    The developer wrote on Reddit (quoted by PC Gamer) that the whole game was vibe-coded with Claude Code, mostly Opus 4.1 and 4.5, with the art generated by ChatGPT. Its Steam AI disclosure says AI was used for the entire code, text, art, sounds and music.

    What people say

    CODEX MORTIS drew wide press attention for billing itself as the first game made 100% with AI, and most of that coverage is skeptical. Writers questioned the 'first' claim and called the art muddy and inconsistent and the trailer weak. Others said it does play like a real survivors-like game. Players have been kinder than the press: its Steam user reviews are Very Positive (81% of 98). Early player reports did mention interface problems.

    People like

    • Plays like a real, complete survivors-like roguelite
    • Steam players rate it well overall
    • Seen as a notable example of what AI coding tools can now build

    Common complaints

    • Muddy, inconsistent art direction
    • The 'first 100% AI game' claim is hard to verify and seen by some as a publicity stunt
    • Little original about it compared with Vampire Survivors
    • Some players reported interface problems and low overall quality
