    About BigText

    BigText describes itself as a free alternative to EmEditor and UltraEdit. It opens very large files (the maker claims 50 GB in under 50 ms using about 100 MB of memory) and searches them with regular expressions at gigabytes per second.

    For day-to-day work it has tabs, multiple cursors, column and block selection (Alt+drag or Shift+Alt+arrows) with a column editor, find in files, a hex view, streaming file compare, syntax highlighting and a folder sidebar with quick open (Ctrl+P). Dark, light and high-contrast themes follow your system setting. Windows gets an installer and a portable zip, and it is also on Scoop.
