    About Stard

    Type any GitHub username and the shop deals cards from that account's starred repos, shuffled afresh for every run. Buy cards onto your shelf, drag them onto your half of the board and press Fight, and every card acts at once. Cards that share a language or topic form sets, ticked off like a pull request's checks, and after rounds 2 and 4 you pick one of three boosts. Five wins finish a run, three losses end it.

    The same site can turn your stars into profile cards for your GitHub README, a yearly "your stars" recap, and an organizer that sorts stars into GitHub Lists and flags dead repos. You can also run the whole thing locally with npx and the GitHub CLI.
