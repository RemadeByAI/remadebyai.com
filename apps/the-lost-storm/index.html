    About The Lost Storm

    You wake in a white mist with a worn journal and walk out into a hall under a night storm. The game is in alpha: the Hall of Time, a hideout with a trader, an auctioneer and a stash, and a boss lair are in. No install or account is needed. It is built with Three.js and TypeScript, with no game engine.
