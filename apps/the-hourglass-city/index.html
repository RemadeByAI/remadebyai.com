    About The Hourglass City

    It's New Meridian, 1957, and you play detective Frank Calder in Act I, The Ordinary Dead. Frank's office, Ferrier Street, the Hotel Mirador, the Nickel Mile and the Blue Comet jazz club are drawn in real time with lighting, rain and neon. Frank keeps clues in his notebook and makes deductions from them.

    Each game is a single HTML file with no libraries, images or audio files: every building, raindrop and letter of the font is drawn in code, and the music is synthesized. The same repo has The Black Sedan, a car chase and fight, and Ravenshore Garden. It needs a keyboard and mouse, and you can download the file to play offline.
