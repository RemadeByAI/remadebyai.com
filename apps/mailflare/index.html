    About Mailflare

    Mailflare gives you personal or shared mailboxes on custom domains, routing rules to store, forward, reject or sort mail, a calendar with time zones and email invitations, a public booking page, and a Drive for storing and sharing files. Built-in AI agents can search mail, draft replies and manage events. You can import mail from IMAP, Gmail Takeout, .eml and .mbox files, and the interface comes in 34 languages.

    It deploys to Cloudflare Workers with one button or runs as a single Docker container, with a choice of databases (Cloudflare D1, PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite and others) and storage (R2, B2 or S3). Receiving through Cloudflare Email Routing is free and sending can use the free tiers of Resend or Amazon SES. A hosted Mailflare Cloud is also offered from $4.
