    About Hack://TheSystem

    You sit at a hacker terminal and type commands to work through four puzzle stages, with Matrix-style effects, glitches and a trace meter that fills as you go. A boss stage, where you type the packets it throws at you before they reach your firewall, was added in an update on 24 October 2026; the maker admits it is hard.

    It takes about 5 to 10 minutes, is played with the keyboard, is in English and runs in the browser (made with Unity). It aims for an unsettling mood rather than jump scares.

    How it was built with AI

    The maker says on itch.io: "This game was entirely made by Claude Opus 5.5." They wrote one opening prompt into an empty project, answered two questions, and "Claude did the rest."
