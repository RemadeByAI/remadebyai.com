    FluidWin

    Alternative to the Windows 11 taskbar

    Reshapes Windows 11 with a dock that replaces the taskbar, a notch that shows the time, volume, music and notifications, and a glass control center. Close it and Windows goes back exactly as it was.

    About FluidWin

    The dock shows your apps with notification badges, live window previews, jump-list commands, a Downloads stack and the Recycle Bin, and can sit on any edge of the screen or follow you to another monitor. The notch at the top grows to show volume, notifications, the song playing with its controls and a timer, and has a shelf you can drag files onto. The control center groups Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, dark theme, keep awake, silent mode, timer, calendar, weather and news.

    It is a portable zip with nothing to install, it never changes Windows system files, and it checks for updates itself. It is a one-person hobby project.
