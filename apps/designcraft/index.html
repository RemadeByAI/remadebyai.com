    About DesignCraft

    DesignCraft lays out magazines, books and flyers with spreads and parent pages, threaded text frames, paragraph and character styles, tables, text wrap and a baseline grid. Its paragraph composer uses the Knuth-Plass algorithm with hyphenation and optical margin alignment, and line breaks match on screen and in PDF.

    It imports and exports IDML, imports INDD files through the maker's own indd-utils library, and exports PNG, PDF (PDF/X-4, PDF/A-2b), EPUB and editable .pptx. No subscription, licence server or telemetry.

    What people say

    Coverage is thin but includes one real hands-on test. An Italian tech blogger tried version 0.8.0 on a Mac. Rendering, PDF/X-4 export, IDML round-trips and mail merge all worked, though he found an Italian hyphenation problem in that build and says .indd support is still unproven. A Hacker News user said it works fine and behaves well, with some things a little different from InDesign.

    People like

    • Fast rendering and working PDF export in a hands-on test
    • IDML files round-trip cleanly with its own documents
    • Runs as a portable app and behaves well

    Common complaints

    • Opening real InDesign .indd files is still unproven
    • IDML compatibility only shown with files it created itself
    • Some behaviour differs from InDesign
