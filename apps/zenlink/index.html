    About ZenLink

    ZenLink mirrors a phone or tablet in a window on Windows: 60 fps on iPhone and iPad and up to 120 fps on Android, with hardware decoding, synced sound and mouse and keyboard control. Android phones (5.0 or newer, sound from Android 11) connect over USB or Wi-Fi using the scrcpy server, with nothing to install on the phone. iPhones and iPads connect through Apple's driver (iOS 17 or newer with Developer Mode, for control) or through ZenLink Studio, which takes picture and sound over the cable or acts as an AirPlay receiver.

    Extras include Alt+C to copy text off the phone screen with OCR, dropping files on the window to send them, launching Android apps in their own window, a stealth mode that dims the phone, and smooth capture in OBS for streamers. It needs 64-bit Windows 10 (1809) or Windows 11, and Apple Devices or iTunes for iPhones.
