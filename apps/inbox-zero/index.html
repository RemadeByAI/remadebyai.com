    About Inbox Zero

    The assistant works in the background on your Gmail or Outlook account. You tell it in plain English how to handle mail, and it labels and files messages, drafts replies in your tone, tracks what you still need to answer, blocks cold emails, bulk-archives and unsubscribes, briefs you before meetings and saves attachments to Google Drive or OneDrive. You can also chat with it from Slack or Telegram.

    The separate email client has a split inbox and all your accounts in one place, on the web and desktop, with iPhone and Android apps. You can self-host it with Docker or use the hosted version at getinboxzero.com.
