    About Unsung Vale

    You play the Cantor, a masked wanderer-knight crossing the Vale at golden hour. Break the constructs that guard each spire and wake it, and colour, sound and people return to that part of the valley. The first three spires stand in the inner valley; the other five sit on mesas around the lake, reached by riding strings of light. A full journey with side quests and trials takes about two hours.

    Everything you see, terrain, trees, buildings and enemies, is generated by code, with no imported models or textures. Needs a recent Chrome or Edge with WebGL 2.
