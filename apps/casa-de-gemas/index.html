    About Casa de Gemas

    Made as a birthday gift for the maker's daughter: a whitewashed Spanish house, a bare courtyard, a watering can and a year of hidden gems. Water the tree bed and a bougainvillea grows, then shake branches, tap pots and explore to find five gems in each of the four seasons, while Oli the owl narrates from the tower roof.

    It's one self-contained HTML file with hand-drawn canvas art, spring physics for every branch and petal, and music generated live. There's a separate tap-only version for phones.
