    About LightCraft

    LightCraft keeps a non-destructive photo library and develops raw files with light, tone curve, colour grading and effects panels, plus before and after views. Your photos stay on your own computer.

    It also ships a CLI and an MCP server so AI agents can drive edits. The project calls itself alpha.
