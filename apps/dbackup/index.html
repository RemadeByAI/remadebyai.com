    About DBackup

    DBackup backs up 10 kinds of database (MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, MongoDB, Redis, Firebird and more, directly or over SSH) together with the files that belong to them, then sends them to 13 kinds of storage such as S3, cloud drives, SFTP, SMB and WebDAV. It does incremental backups with daily, weekly, monthly and yearly retention, and can restore a whole database or a single file.

    It runs as one Docker container. Backups are standard dumps and TAR archives encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and a Recovery Kit decrypts them without DBackup. It has SSO, permissions, 2FA, passkeys and an audit log for teams.
