    About Aevum

    Build a village, gather resources, raise an army and knock down your rival across five ages, from the Stone Age to the Industrial Age. Play against the computer or a friend online, with a sandbox setup and spectators.

    It runs in the browser with Three.js and no build step; you can also run it yourself with Node.js. The game text is in Turkish. It is very new and updated often.
