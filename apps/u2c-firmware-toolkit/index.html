    About U2C Firmware Toolkit

    Out of the box, macOS sees the Ultimate 2C as a generic device that most Mac games ignore. This app installs 8BitDo's own firmware 1.09 with tweaks you pick, so the controller presents itself as an Xbox controller by cable, over Bluetooth and through the 2.4 GHz dongle. It also adds rumble over Bluetooth, a dimmable Home ring, battery alerts and fewer drop-outs, and lets you change dead zones, trigger ranges and button layout without flashing again.

    It backs up the controller and dongle before writing anything and can restore the backup or the original firmware at any time. Only for the Ultimate 2C on firmware 1.09 and its dongle on 1.03. Modified firmware may void the warranty; the README's disclaimer explains the risks.
