    Inspired by turn-based puzzle games

    About Coalheart

    Torches never refill and every room holds exactly the fire it needs. You bump a torch to take its fire, spend 3 to light a brazier that opens the gate with the same rune and 2 to burn away brambles, all while a gauge limits how much you can carry. If the fire left can no longer reach the stairs, the game tells you, and undo and restart are free.

    There are 15 rooms in three chapters, each with a par for the fewest moves, and your best results are kept in the browser. It plays with arrow keys or WASD, or by tapping and swiping on a touch screen. The pixel art, fonts, music and sound are all made in code.

    How it was built with AI

    The maker's AI disclosure on itch.io: "Coalheart was made with Claude Code, Anthropic's AI coding assistant, under the developer's direction." In a comment the maker adds that he described the concept and style, "gave all this to Claude and I got the fully working game" (https://nebitula.itch.io/coalheart).

    What people say

    The feedback comes from players on itch.io, mostly fellow entrants in the AI Browser Game Jam 5, where it tied for first place overall from 7 ratings. Players praise the pixel art, the music and how cleanly each room teaches one idea. The main criticisms are that the room is drawn small on a large screen, par counts every step so early rooms feel like route tracing, and later puzzles could make more use of the level layout. Coverage is limited to jam comments.

    People like

    • Charming pixel art and music
    • Each room teaches one idea clearly
    • Free undo and restart, and par scores that invite replays

    Common complaints

    • The room is drawn small, so tiles can be tiny
    • Early rooms feel more like tracing a route than solving
    • Later puzzles could use the level layout more
