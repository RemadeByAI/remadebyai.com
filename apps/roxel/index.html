    About Roxel

    Roxel gives you a big open canvas and a small floating toolbar: brush, erase, recolor, eyedropper, shapes (boxes, spheres, cylinders, lines), selection with copy and paste, and a move tool. It comes with palettes such as PICO-8, Sweetie 16 and Endesga 32, lets you make your own, and has light and dark themes and a command palette with every shortcut.

    It opens MagicaVoxel .vox, Qubicle .qb and Goxel .gox files, and exports to .vox, .gox, OBJ, glTF (ready for Unity or Godot), transparent PNG and SVG, plus a lit, framed "Roxel Shot" image for sharing. A separate rox command-line tool can render and convert models without the app. Builds for Windows, macOS and Linux are on GitHub and itch.io; they are not signed, so macOS and Windows warn on first launch.
