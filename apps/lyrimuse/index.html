    About Lyrimuse

    Lyrimuse lives in the menu bar and highlights lyrics word by word as a song plays, for Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube Music, Amazon Music and many other players, including Chinese-language ones such as QQ Music and NetEase. It searches sixteen lyric sources automatically, scores each match, and can show translations and romanization under the original. Duets are split by singer, and a Lyrics Manager lets you fix or re-match any track.

    It also scrobbles to Last.fm and shows your listening stats and a yearly heatmap. The maker pitches it at people coming from LyricsX, which has had no release since April 2022. It needs macOS 14 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and installs with Homebrew or from the releases page. It is free, with no paid version or account.
