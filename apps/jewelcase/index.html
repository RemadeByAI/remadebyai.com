    About Jewelcase

    Your game library lives on the PC; plug in the handheld and Jewelcase shows everything in one list with size, play count and play time. A tick means the game should be on the device, and Sync makes it so. There is a cover-art grid, smart lists such as Never Played and Largest on Device, and an inbox that works out which system a new download is for and converts archives to CHD or RVZ where the handheld uses them.

    It also shows which emulators are installed and which one ES-DE uses for each system, checks for the BIOS files they need, backs up saves and save states whenever the handheld is connected, and shows battery and storage. It was built for an Anbernic RG477V running ES-DE but should work with any Android handheld that keeps games in per-system folders. It ships as an AppImage and needs adb. You bring your own games.

    How it was built with AI

    The README says Jewelcase "was written entirely by Claude"; a person decided what it should do and tested it on their handheld but wrote none of the code, and docs/how-this-was-built.md tells the story. Checked 2026-11-28: 2 of 16 commits carry Claude co-author lines.
