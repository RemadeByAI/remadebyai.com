    About Mila

    Mila records your microphone, the audio of one app such as Zoom, Slack or Google Meet in Chrome, or both together for meetings, with no virtual audio device to install. It transcribes with Whisper through whisper.cpp on the Mac's GPU, labels who said what and remembers speaker names, and exports SRT subtitles. Global shortcuts dictate English or Hebrew into any app, and voice memos synced from your iPhone are transcribed automatically. Titles and summaries can come from an AI tool you already use, and are off until you turn them on.

    It needs macOS 14 or later; an Apple silicon Mac with 16 GB of memory is recommended, and the two default models take about 4.6 GB. Companies can instead point every Mac at their own transcription server and roll out settings through MDM or a shared config file.
