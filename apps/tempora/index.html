    About Tempora

    Start life in a Natufian hunting camp, ancient Thebes or a colony in the asteroid belt, across eleven eras and about 200 lands. Go to school, build a career, marry, raise children and carry on as one of them. Meanwhile the world keeps simulating everyone: countries rise and fall, cities grow, migrations move people and rulers start wars.

    It's one HTML file that works offline and installs as an app on phone or desktop, with no ads or accounts. Optional Claude features (chatting with characters, advice, story write-ups) need your own API key; the rest of the game doesn't.
