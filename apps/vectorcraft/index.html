    About VectorCraft

    VectorCraft covers artboards, Bézier pen and direct selection tools, Pathfinder, live blends, clipping groups, appearance effects such as glows, and 3D Revolve.

    Like the rest of the suite it has a CLI and an MCP server, so Claude and other agents can draw, edit and export artwork.
