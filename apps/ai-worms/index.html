    About AI WORMS

    You chase the worms from the Kitchen through the Living Room, Garage, Yard, a Halloween campfire, Dracula's Castle, Outer Space, the Glowing Deep and Neon Tokyo up into the Cloud for a boss fight, with a Christmas bonus level after it. Tapping friendly characters gives a bonus, linked worms pop together, and catching bouncing worms or flying treats doubles your points. Let five worms escape and you can continue from the level you reached.

    It is made for ages 7 and up and plays best on a phone in portrait; on a laptop a yellow ring shows which worm your cursor will hit, and desktop play is deliberately easier. There are no image or audio files: all graphics, music and sound are drawn and synthesised live in the browser.

    How it was built with AI

    The maker says on itch.io: "built entirely by AI, directed by a human. Every line of code, every drawing, every sound and all the music came from Claude (Anthropic) through conversation." They playtested over more than 140 versions, and Claude also wrote test bots that play the levels.

    What people say

    Feedback comes from players on itch.io, mostly other entrants in the AI Browser Game Jam 5, where it was rated by 29 players. People describe it as a cute, silly and chaotic whack-a-mole with surprisingly many levels and satisfying sound effects. Criticisms are that later stages get too busy to read, it can feel repetitive, and one player found it too fast on desktop (the maker has since made desktop easier). Coverage is limited to jam comments, which tend to be friendly.
