    About Concat

    Concat covers what most edits need: multi-track cutting, titles and shapes, more than 100 effects and transitions, speed ramps, keyframes with a curve editor, masks, chroma key, stabilizing and voiceover with one-switch voice cleanup. It exports H.264, HEVC and AV1 up to 4K 60 using your computer's own encoder.

    The AI parts (auto-captions, voice isolation, text-to-speech and cutouts) download once and then run offline on your machine, so your footage never leaves your disk. It is good for TikTok, Reels, Shorts and YouTube videos. The iOS build is sideloaded.

    What people say

    Concat got enthusiastic coverage from tech sites in several languages as a free, open source CapCut alternative with no watermark, account or paywall. A Polish hands-on review found it intuitive and quick, but said automatic subtitles sometimes misrecognise speech or fail entirely, and that it will not replace advanced editors.
