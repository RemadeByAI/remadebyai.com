    About Ironvale

    Play the four-chapter story campaign, The Shattering of Ironvale, or a skirmish on one of five map types, from the Green Vale to the Iron Highlands and the Corsair Coast. You gather resources, build a town, advance through three ages and raise an army.

    It runs in a desktop browser or as one portable Windows exe that works offline, and is made for mouse and keyboard. The 3D models are openly licensed (CC0 and CC-BY) works by other artists. The repo has no licence file, so the code is visible but not open source.
