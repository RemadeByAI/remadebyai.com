    About Voidstruck

    A jump drive test goes wrong and throws you 2,500 light years from home. Every star, planet, moon and name is worked out from a seed as you arrive, and you can fly down through the clouds, land and walk among plants and animals. Build bases that earn while you travel, trade for upgrades and fight or flee raiders.

    There are no images, models or sound files: the pictures are WebGPU shaders and the ten-piece soundtrack is synthesised in the browser. It is best in a current Chrome or Edge on a desktop with a mouse and keyboard, and the single file also works offline.

    How it was built with AI

    What people say

    Coverage is thin and very recent: an enthusiastic XDA Developers article and a WindowsForum piece that largely builds on it. Both were impressed that a whole procedurally generated galaxy of 150 million star systems fits in one small HTML file. WindowsForum added the caveats that it needs a WebGPU-capable browser, so a tiny file does not mean it runs everywhere, and that it is better described as AI-assisted and human-directed than AI-made.
