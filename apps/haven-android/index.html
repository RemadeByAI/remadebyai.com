    About Haven for Android

    Haven gathers terminal and remote-desktop tools on an Android phone or tablet: SSH and Mosh, VNC, RDP and SPICE desktops, SFTP and SMB file access, cloud storage, email, a local Linux shell and even a full Linux kernel in user mode, plus mesh networking. The terminal and screens can be made translucent over your wallpaper.
