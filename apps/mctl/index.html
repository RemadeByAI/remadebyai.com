    About mctl

    mctl follows the ISS and the Dragons that visit it, Hubble, JWST, Roman, Tiangong, the Voyagers and New Horizons, and is styled like a flight control room monitor: a 3D orbit map, ground track, sky plot, pass predictions, a maneuver planner and live ISS telemetry. It has seven themes, from green phosphor to Teletext.

    It is written in Rust and tested on Kubuntu and a Raspberry Pi 3B+, with a low-resolution mode for feeding an old CRT.
