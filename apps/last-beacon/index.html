    About Last Beacon

    Mechanical creatures crawl out of the sea toward your lighthouse. Turrets only fire when they're connected to the beacon through other buildings or relays, and limited grid capacity decides which ones get power. The beacon is a weapon too: point its beam at the ground to light up enemies so they take more damage and slow down, which matters on night waves when turrets can't see far.

    Four turret types, relays, overdrive and an area strike, weather that turns to storm by wave eight, ten waves and a final boss, then an endless mode. It's in English and Chinese.
