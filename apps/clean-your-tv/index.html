    Clean Your TV

    Alternative to the stock Google TV home screen

    Strips the ads, tracking and preloaded bloat from an Android TV or Google TV over Wi-Fi and swaps in a clean, Apple TV-style home screen. No root, nothing uninstalled, and one command undoes it all.

    About Clean Your TV

    Android TVs have the same debug bridge as Android phones. Clean Your TV uses it over Wi-Fi to back up your settings, switch off the maker's telemetry, ad services, sponsored rows and preloaded apps (switched off, not deleted), and install a small native launcher as the home screen. Its lists cover 504 TV models from 138 brands.

    The usual way to run it is to hand the project's prompt to a coding assistant such as Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI on a computer on the same Wi-Fi; it finds the bloat on your TV and asks before touching anything you might use. You can also run the same steps by hand. There is no app-store app and no prebuilt APK. Samsung, LG, Roku, Vizio and Hisense VIDAA TVs are not supported.

    It started on 17 October 2026 as a before-and-after post of the maker's parents' TV that reached 8.6M views. It was the #1 trending repository on GitHub for the week of 19 to 25 October and had about 60,000 stars by late November, with coverage in TechCrunch, TechRadar, Tom's Guide, Android Authority, XDA and hundreds more.

    How it was built with AI

    The maker says the first TV was "cleaned up by Claude Code over Wi-Fi", and the project is designed to be run by a coding assistant. Checked 2026-11-22 in the repo's commit history: 1,136 of 6,869 commits are co-authored by Claude, and the repo has an AGENTS.md file.
