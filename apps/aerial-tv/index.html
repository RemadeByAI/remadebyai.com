    About Aerial

    Aerial replaces the advert-heavy home screens on Xiaomi, TCL, Skyworth, Sony and other TVs sold in China, and runs on boxes and projectors too. Your favourite apps sit on a glass shelf with everything else below, you can arrange tiles from your phone by scanning a code, and a Control Center gathers inputs, network, volume and brightness. Screensavers play Apple TV's aerial films, your photos or a clock.

    It installs with ADB (no root, nothing removed, and it can be undone), and starts at boot. The main README is in Chinese, with an English version.
