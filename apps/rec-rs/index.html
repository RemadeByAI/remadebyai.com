    About rec-rs

    rec-rs records the screen, system audio, microphone and webcam into a single .mov or .mp4 that opens in QuickTime, Final Cut, Premiere and DaVinci Resolve, or writes screen and camera as separate files in sync. The webcam overlay can be dragged anywhere and shaped as a circle, squircle or pill. It can also keep a replay buffer of the last seconds and stream over RTMP (YouTube, Twitch, Kick) or WHIP to several places at once.

    It uses only Apple frameworks (ScreenCaptureKit, VideoToolbox, Metal), with no FFmpeg, OBS or Electron inside, and needs macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. The maker says it is a clean-room rewrite: OBS was studied for its architecture with the reverse-engineering tool REA and Claude Code, and no OBS code was copied.
