    About Bee Garden

    You're a honeybee forager on a summer's day: fly among poppies, daisies and cornflowers, land on their moving petals, gather nectar and pollen, shelter from the weather and get home before dark. A full game is five summers, one queen's reign. What you bring home fills the hive's stores for the winters, and what you pollinate shapes next summer's meadow.

    Play on a desktop or laptop with keyboard and mouse, or on a tablet held sideways. It isn't set up for phones yet.
