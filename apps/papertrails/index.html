    About Papertrails

    A calm walking game made of card and paper. As the traveller walks on, the world folds up out of the ground like a pop-up book: autumn forests, villages, desert, snow, and day turning into night, when the village windows light up.

    There's no goal to chase. It runs in the browser on three.js.
