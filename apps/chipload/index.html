    Chipload

    Alternative to Vectric VCarve and Carbide Create

    Free 2.5D CAM software for CNC routers and mills: bring in a DXF or SVG drawing (or draw in the app), set up cuts, simulate them in 3D and export checked G-code.

    About Chipload

    Chipload turns drawings into toolpaths: profiles with tabs and dogbones, pockets with islands, V-carving (including text), chamfers, drilling and boring, fill patterns, mouldings, surfacing and 3D reliefs carved from an STL model or a depth-map picture. A material-removal simulation shows the cut in 3D with cycle time and chip load, it can nest many parts onto boards, and an independent checker reads every program for machine limits, rapids through the stock and other mistakes before the G-code is written.

    The maker says its programs have cut wood on a GRBL router (a Shapeoko) but have not yet been tried on LinuxCNC or Mach3/4, or in inches, and asks people with real machines to test it, starting with an air cut. It is written in Rust, with downloads for Linux, Windows and macOS.
