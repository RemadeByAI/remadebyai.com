    About No Sleep

    One toggle stops the Mac sleeping when idle or when you shut the lid. Timers sit right in the menu (15 minutes to 8 hours, or type something like 1h30m or 9am), and it can stay awake only while Claude Code, Codex or T3 Code are working.

    Safety cut-outs switch it off if the Mac runs hot for 20 minutes, if the internet has been down too long, or when the battery gets low; it can fall back to your phone's hotspot and dim the screen to black without locking. It is SwiftUI with no dependencies, under 1 MB, for Apple Silicon on macOS 14 and later, in English and Spanish. It isn't notarized yet, so the first launch needs one Terminal command from the README.
