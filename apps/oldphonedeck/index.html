    About OldPhoneDeck

    A menu bar app on the Mac (PhoneDeck.app) has a Command Center where you drag apps, widgets and controls onto pages for an iPhone 4S, a modern iPhone or an iPad mini. Keys open any Mac app with its Dock badge, control Spotify or Apple Music, volume, brightness, dark mode, window tiling, Focus and even an external monitor's input. Widgets include clock, weather, calendar, Mac performance, batteries and handwritten notes.

    It works on devices as old as an iPhone 4S on iOS 9, and a phone can also serve as a webcam and mic for calls or as a small second display. You build the Mac app from source with one script (macOS 14 or later and the Xcode command line tools).
