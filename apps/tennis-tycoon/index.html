    About Tennis Tycoon

    $2.99 on the App Store, with optional in-app purchases that aren't needed to progress and no ads or waiting timers. Its maker had no development experience before this game. An Android version is planned.

    How it was built with AI

    MobileGamer.biz reports that Iain Atkinson, a retired tennis pro, made the game entirely with Claude Code, and that it wouldn't exist without it.

    What people say

    Tennis Tycoon has a small but positive set of App Store ratings, and players call it addictive and fun. Mobilegamer.biz ran an interview with its developer, a retired tennis pro, which focuses on how he built it rather than judging the game. One reviewer liked it but said it still has a lot of flaws. Coverage is thin so far.
