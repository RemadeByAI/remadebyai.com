    About Quarter Sun Cove

    Quarter Sun Cove (first called Pumpkin Farmer) is a relaxed farming sim seen from an isometric view. You plant and grow pumpkins and get to know the animal characters who live around the cove. Its maker set out to make something original rather than an imitation of an existing farming game.

    The written spec it was built from is published on GitHub, and the story of how it was made is on simonwillison.net.

    How it was built with AI

    Simon Willison wrote that he had Claude Opus 5.5 work for nearly two days building it from his spec (simonwillison.net, 2026-10-28).
