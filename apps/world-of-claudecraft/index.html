    About World of ClaudeCraft

    Climb from Eastbrook Vale through marshes and mountains to level-cap frontiers, with talent trees, instanced dungeons with a Heroic tier, world bosses, a ten-player raid and procedural rift portals for the endgame. There are mounts, ten crafts fed by four gathering trades, a player market, guilds, a dungeon finder and 1v1 and 2v2 arena ladders.

    Terrain, water, weather and towns are generated in code at runtime, and the game is translated into 22 languages. You can host your own world with one command, or train AI agents to play it through a headless Gym interface.

    The game has an optional crypto token ($WOC) for cosmetic extras and wallet flair. The makers say it never sells power, and you can play without it.

    What people say

    World of ClaudeCraft drew wide attention in mid 2026 as an MMO built largely with an AI coding agent in a few days, reportedly reaching over 30,000 players. Writers and Hacker News commenters were impressed by the scope for a weekend project and called it a fascinating proof of concept. Critics say it is far from polished, call the generated code messy and hard to maintain, report performance drops, and point out that many assets come from existing packs. Much of the coverage is about the AI angle more than the game itself.

    People like

    • Remarkable scope for a project built in days
    • Free to play in a browser on desktop and phone

    Common complaints

    • Feels unpolished and far behind the games it imitates
    • Developers criticise the generated code as messy and hard to maintain
    • Some players report performance drops
    • Relies on existing asset packs, so less AI-made than it looks
