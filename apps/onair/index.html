    OnAir

    Alternative to SDR# and SDR++

    Turns a cheap software-defined radio into a receiver for digital TV, DAB radio, aircraft, ships, weather satellites, pagers and much more, with the spectrum and decoded data in one app.

    About OnAir

    Plug in an RTL-SDR, HackRF, Airspy, SDRplay, LimeSDR, Pluto or one of many other radios, pick a mode and press Start. OnAir has close to 400 receive modes: digital TV (DVB-T/T2, ATSC, ISDB-T) with a live player, DAB+ and FM with RDS, ADS-B aircraft and AIS ships on a map, NOAA and Meteor weather satellite pictures, DMR, P25 and TETRA, pagers, FT8 and other ham modes, and sensors. It shows the spectrum, waterfall, constellations and a signal quality score, with auto-tune, gain help and a channel scanner. No radio yet? A built-in tour plays a demo signal.

    Installers are on GitHub for macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel, not signed), 64-bit Windows 10 and 11, Linux (x86-64 and arm64) and Android 9 or later (receive only). Windows users need the WinUSB driver for HackRF, RTL-SDR and Airspy radios. Patented voice codecs are not included. Many newer decoders have been tested only on generated signals, so some may not work on the air yet.

    What people say

    RTL-SDR.com, the main radio hobby blog, covered OnAir twice in October 2026, and its readers' comments give a mixed but mostly warm picture. Fans call it brilliant and one of the few useful AI-built projects in the hobby. Others report that digital TV decoding failed for them, call it buggy, or would rather see the basics polished before more decoders are added. The blog itself cautions that decoders written this quickly may not all work as expected.
