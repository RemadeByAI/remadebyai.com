    About Capso

    Capso puts area, fullscreen, window, scrolling, timer, OCR and recording captures in one floating toolbar. Screenshots open in an annotation editor with arrows, shapes, text, highlighter, numbered markers, pixelate and crop, and a beautify mode adds a background, padding, rounded corners and a shadow. You can pin captures above other windows, copy text with OCR, translate a screen area, and browse everything in a history window.

    It records MP4 or GIF with system audio, microphone and a webcam bubble in four shapes, and a recording editor handles trimming, zoom suggestions, cursor smoothing and backgrounds. Optional cloud sharing uploads to your own Cloudflare R2 bucket. It is a native Swift app for macOS 15 or later, available as a signed DMG or with Homebrew (brew install --cask capso), and is translated into 12 languages.
