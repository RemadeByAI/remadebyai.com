    About FigCraft

    FigCraft has frames, auto layout, components and variants, vector networks, styles, variables and click-through prototypes in a native app that opens instantly and works offline. It has a first preview of live multiplayer sessions hosted from the app or your own server.

    Its README is frank about the gaps: it estimates it is about 49% of the way to replacing proprietary tools, opens .fig files only on a best-effort basis, and has no accounts or cloud yet.

    What people say

    FigCraft is covered as an early, open source Figma alternative. Writers like the idea of owning both the editor and its infrastructure and note improving Figma imports, but stress it is alpha, very rough, and lacks user accounts and proven collaboration, which limits team use. Coverage is thin and mostly cautious.

    People like

    • Easier imports of existing Figma designs
    • Full control over the editor and its hosting
    • Free and open source

    Common complaints

    • Still alpha and rough around the edges
    • Missing user accounts and unproven collaboration hold back team adoption
    • Import fidelity not yet checked against real Figma files
