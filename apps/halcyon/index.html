    About Halcyon

    Everything is drawn live with three.js and WebGL2: an FFT ocean that breaks on the reef, a 330 m peak built from real lidar, 43,000 scanned plants and 370,000 coral colonies, whales, dolphins, turtles and schools of fish, and a 36-minute day whose weather changes by itself before the lagoon lights up with bioluminescence.

    It is a calm place to explore rather than a game with goals, made to the standard of a game's hero shot and kept at 60 fps on a laptop. It is from the maker of Neon Zenith and Sakura Fantasy. The code is MIT; models, textures and fonts keep their own licences.
