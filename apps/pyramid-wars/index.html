    About Pyramid Wars

    You lead a blue army across 10 desert and winter battlefields, placing factories, towers and crystal harvesters, dragging units into position and casting abilities on keys 1 to 5. Gold earned in battle buys booster packs of unit and ability cards (common, rare and legendary) that strengthen your army, and there is a tutorial and a map editor.

    The free browser version on itch.io is a demo that won the AI Browser Game Jam 3. A Steam version for Windows and Linux has a planned release date of 1 March 2027, with a demo and a playtest already on Steam (store.steampowered.com/app/4996430).

    How it was built with AI

    The maker says on itch.io: "This was vibe coded using Claude Opus 4.8 with a 20x Pro plan in 3 weeks. It's vanilla HTML + JS. The sounds and graphics were also created with Claude and are included in the code." The jam entry adds that the cover image was made with Super Grok.

    What people say

    The only feedback so far comes from players on itch.io, mostly fellow entrants in the AI Browser Game Jam 3, where it ranked first overall from 14 ratings. Players enjoyed the fast matches, the depth and the tutorial, and several called it their favourite entry. The common criticism is balance: difficulty jumps after level 5, the tank AI wanders, and the mix of clicker and RTS can feel busy. Coverage is thin and limited to jam comments.

    People like

    • Fast, action-packed matches
    • Deeper strategy than expected for a jam game
    • Clear tutorial that helps newcomers to the genre

    Common complaints

    • Difficulty spikes after level 5
    • Tank AI sometimes ignores the base
    • Clicker and RTS parts compete, which can feel cluttered
