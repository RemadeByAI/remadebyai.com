    About Lucrometro

    A watchlist shows each ticker's last price and today's change. Tap one for its chart, from today's session back to its full history, and add your average buy price to see how far it has moved, marked on the chart. While the market is open the details screen can refresh every 15 seconds.

    Everything stays on the phone. When a price can't be fetched the app says so and shows the last quote as stale rather than wrong data. It is in English and Italian, is not on Google Play, and installs as a signed APK (Obtainium can keep it updated).
