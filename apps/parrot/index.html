    About Parrot

    Parrot records your Mac's system audio and your microphone as two tracks, so it works with Zoom, Google Meet, Teams or anything else your Mac can hear, and no bot joins the call. Transcription runs on the Mac with Whisper or Parakeet, it tells speakers apart, and afterwards it writes a report with a summary, objections, talk balance and the next steps people promised. You can search all your past calls and ask questions about them.

    The live assistant is optional. You choose what powers it: Claude with your own API key, any OpenAI-compatible server, or a local model through Ollama, which keeps everything offline and free. You can add PDFs or notes, and it suggests answers from them while the other side is still talking. Parrot needs macOS 14 or later on an Apple silicon Mac. Releases are still marked as pre-releases, and the maker says some bugs remain.

    What people say

    Coverage is thin: a Show HN thread from September 2026 (39 points), a few comments on Product Hunt and some thank-you notes on GitHub. People like that no bot joins the call, that it's free and open source, and how polished the setup feels. An early tester reported an echo on the other side's voice in recordings, and one commenter felt there are already plenty of meeting recorders. Several found the assistant's old name, Copilot, confusing; the maker renamed it Assistant the next day.
