    About Lanternfall

    Fights run on their own while you pick the party (a warden, a reaver, a fletcher and a lamplighter among them), spend loot and relics to level and re-equip them, and fire a flare at the right moment to light the field and boost the heroes when a wave or a boss gets hard. Game speed goes up to 4x.

    The whole game, models, animation and music included, is one self-contained HTML page built on three.js.
