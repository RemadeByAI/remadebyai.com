    About AppGrid for Plasma

    AppGrid replaces Plasma's Kickoff and Kicker menus with a centered grid of your apps, or a panel popup that uses the same grid. Search finds apps and KRunner results as you type, and you can sort by category, pin favourites and change the look in its settings.

    It is packaged by its maker for Arch (AUR), Ubuntu 25.10+ (PPA) and Fedora (Copr), with a universal package for immutable distros such as Bazzite and SteamOS and a Nix flake. After installing, right-click the panel launcher and pick Show Alternatives.

    What people say

    AppGrid got coverage on Linux news sites at launch in March 2026 and on later releases, plus discussion on the EndeavourOS and KDE forums. Users like how it looks and that it offers a Launchpad-style option for Plasma. Complaints are mostly early teething problems: install errors, a blank window for one user, a breakage after the Plasma 6.7 update with a beta theme, and some who prefer the traditional KDE menu. Press coverage is mostly descriptive release news.
