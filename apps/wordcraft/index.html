    How it was built with AI

    Checked 2026-11-22 in the repo's commit history: 1,674 of 3,580 commits are co-authored by Claude, and the repo has CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md files. XDA lists it as a vibe-coded Word replacement.

    What people say

    WordCraft got tech press coverage as part of ArtCraft's launch of free Rust rewrites of the Office apps. The Register tried it and found it worked fairly well, and other outlets called the suite promising. All of them said it is early software and not yet a dependable replacement for Word. Most coverage is about the suite as a whole and how it was built with AI, not detailed hands-on testing of WordCraft.

    People like

    • Ran fairly well in a quick hands-on test on an Arm-based Windows laptop
    • Familiar Word-like layout, and it opens and saves .docx files
    • Seen as a promising free, open-source option

    Common complaints

    • Reviewers advise keeping it away from real work for now
    • Unproven with complex real-world documents, macros and collaboration
    • Few detailed hands-on reviews of WordCraft on its own
