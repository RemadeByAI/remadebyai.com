    What people say

    micasa drew a lively Show HN discussion and a handful of write-ups on Linux and terminal tool sites. People praise the polished terminal interface, the documentation and the local, single-file SQLite approach with no cloud account. Critics on Hacker News doubted that family members would use a terminal app, questioned trusting important home records to an AI-assisted codebase, and wondered whether it beats a plain spreadsheet. The blog coverage is mostly descriptive and enthusiastic rather than critical.

    People like

    • Attractive, well-designed terminal interface and documentation
    • Keeps everything local in one SQLite file, with no account or subscription
    • Covers maintenance schedules, warranties, projects and vendors in one place

    Common complaints

    • A terminal app is hard to share with non-technical household members
    • Some are wary of trusting important records to AI-assisted code
    • Hard to justify over a free spreadsheet for some users
