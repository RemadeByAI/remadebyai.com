    About Hadron

    Hadron sets up the Mac version of Steam so Windows games install and launch from your library as usual. Nothing runs under Rosetta: Wine is built natively for arm64, FEX translates the game's x86 code, and Direct3D, Vulkan and OpenGL are translated to Metal, with Retina mode and MetalFX upscaling.

    It needs macOS 27 on Apple Silicon and installs with Homebrew or a disk image. It is early: a handful of games play well and many don't start yet, and the project keeps a list of which ones work. It is unofficial and not affiliated with Valve, Apple or CodeWeavers.
