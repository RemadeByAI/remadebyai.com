    About YAME

    Open a folder or drop MP3s on the window to edit title, artist, album, album artist, track number, year and genre, for one song or many at once. Cover art can be dropped, pasted or taken from a YouTube link's thumbnail, then cropped or fitted to a square and saved as a 500 by 500 baseline JPEG, the format car head units read most reliably.

    Track numbers follow the list by themselves: add or reorder songs and the numbers in the files update. The folder view refreshes as files change in another app.

    How it was built with AI

    The README says every line of YAME (the code, tests, icon, build scripts and the README) was written by Claude Opus 5.5 in a single conversation, while a human described what they wanted and tested it.
