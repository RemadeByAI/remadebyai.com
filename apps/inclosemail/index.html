    About InCloseMail

    InCloseMail was checked against classic Outlook screen by screen: the ribbon, folder pane, reading pane and To-Do Bar, Outlook's keyboard shortcuts and right-click menus, .pst import and export, rules and Quick Steps, calendar, people and tasks, shared mailboxes and Microsoft 365 Groups.

    It adds things Outlook doesn't have: AI agents such as Claude can read and draft mail in the mailboxes you choose, Clean Up My Inbox sorts thousands of messages into groups you can act on at once, you can change any keyboard shortcut, and it keeps working offline during a Microsoft 365 outage.

    It is free forever for Outlook.com, Hotmail, Live and other personal Microsoft accounts. Microsoft 365 work accounts will cost $3 per user per month ($36 a year) after the beta, and are free during it. It runs on 64-bit Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) and Windows 11.
