    About Lipflow

    Hold the right Option key, mouth what you want to say with no sound, and let go: Lipflow watches your mouth through the webcam, reads your lips with an on-device model and pastes the text into whatever app you're in. First setup takes about eight minutes: it asks for permissions, has you mouth 24 practice sentences and fine-tunes itself to your face and phrasing (and can learn from your Wispr Flow history).

    It is built for Macs first (Apple Silicon for the best results) and also runs on Windows and Linux. An optional cleanup pass with Claude, Codex or a local Ollama model fixes words lip reading gets wrong. Setup is from source with a script, so it suits people comfortable with a terminal.
