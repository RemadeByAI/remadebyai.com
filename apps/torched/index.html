    About Torched!

    You are the new cook at Chez Flambeau. Hold the mouse button or your finger to torch, keep the flame moving until the whole surface turns amber, then ring the bell to serve. Five nights bring crème brûlées with guests' wishes (extra dark, light, half and half), a side of salmon whose thin tail cooks faster, a lemon meringue tart, marshmallow squares that can catch fire, and a night in the dining car of a train where curves and brakes move the plate under your torch. After each night the critic's review appears as a newspaper clipping, read aloud, with zero to three stars.

    A guest book unlocks special orders for eight regular guests, an endless mode keeps five tables going until you lose three, and there are nine stamps to collect. A night takes about three minutes, it works with mouse or touch, and your progress stays in your browser.

    What people say

    The feedback comes from players on itch.io, mostly fellow entrants in the AI Browser Game Jam 5, where it tied for 4th place overall from 24 ratings. Players like the satisfying colour change of the sugar, the voices and sound, and the double meaning of the theme, and several called it polished. Criticism is mild: the first dish is the hardest, the hint to keep the torch moving comes too late, and the core action can feel slow or repetitive after a few plates. Coverage is limited to jam comments.

    People like

    • Very satisfying sugar that browns exactly where the flame goes
    • Good voice acting and sound design
    • Polished and a clever take on the jam theme
    • Simple to pick up with room to get better

    Common complaints

    • Easy to burn everything; three stars are hard to earn
    • Crème brûlée is the hardest dish but comes first
    • Torching can feel slow or tedious after several dishes
