    About DeckCraft

    DeckCraft builds decks with themes, layouts, shapes, pictures, tables, charts, transitions and animations, opens and saves .pptx files, imports older .ppt files, and presents full screen.

    Its README says it covers 84% of the features it tracks against the app it replaces, and that it is in alpha heading for beta.

    How it was built with AI

    Checked 2026-11-22 in the repo's commit history: 533 of 2,278 commits are co-authored by Claude, and the repo has CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md files. MakeUseOf (Oct 2026) reports the office apps were built with Claude Opus 5.5.

    What people say

    Coverage is thin. One Italian tech blogger wrote a guide based on the project's own docs, and he praises how openly it is developed. He also warns that compatibility with real-world .pptx files is unproven, so anyone who gets decks from other people should wait. On Hacker News one user said it works very well for their PDF export workflow. A consultancy guide recommends keeping it to pilot projects for now.

    People like

    • Development happens in the open with an active issue tracker
    • Works well for at least one user's PDF export workflow
    • Free alternative to PowerPoint across many platforms

    Common complaints

    • Compatibility with real-world .pptx files is unverified
    • SmartArt, macros, and legacy .ppt files are not properly supported
    • Still alpha, so best kept to trials
