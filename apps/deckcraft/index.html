About DeckCraft

DeckCraft builds decks with themes, layouts, shapes, pictures, tables, charts, transitions and animations, opens and saves .pptx files, imports older .ppt files, and presents full screen.

Its README says it covers 84% of the features it tracks against the app it replaces, and that it is in alpha heading for beta.
