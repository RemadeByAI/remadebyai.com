    About LUMENRIFT

    Rifts bleed shadows onto the land, and each night more of them crawl toward the Beacon in the middle of the map. Place walls to re-route them (they follow a live flow field), build towers to burn them away, run crystal harvesters to pay for it all, and ascend the Beacon to unlock new tiers of weapons. Hold out for thirty nights, with three boss nights.

    There are six campaigns (Night, Day and four seasons), each with its own enemies, structures, landscape and music, plus aimable powers and procedural maps. It's TypeScript on a bare canvas, with every sound synthesized, and it works on phones too.
