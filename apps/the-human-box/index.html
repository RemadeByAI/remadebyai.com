    About The Human Box

    Autonomous people are born with traits from both parents, learn, form tribes, build homes and cities, trade, found religions, sign treaties and go to war, from the Stone Age through 45 eras into speculative far-future ones. Each person has their own thoughts, memories, family tree and words of their tribe's language, all written by the simulation itself rather than a language model. You can watch at up to 5000x speed, follow anyone, read the world's chronicles, or use about 160 god tools to answer prayers, reshape land and send plagues or disasters.

    The simulation is Rust compiled to WebAssembly and runs on your own device with no sign-up; worlds save locally and can be exported. Desktop apps for Windows, Linux and Apple silicon Macs are on the releases page; they are not code-signed yet, so you have to get past the security warning.
