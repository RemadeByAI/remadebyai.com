    About MoePeek

    MoePeek is a native Swift app of about 5 MB. It translates selected text, a captured screen region (OCR), the clipboard or manual input, and can show several services side by side: Google, Bing and Youdao for free, DeepL and others with an API key, AI models including local Ollama and LM Studio, and Apple's on-device translation on macOS 15 and newer.
