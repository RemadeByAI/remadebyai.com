    About Summer Festival

    You play a girl in a summer yukata on a street of food and game stalls under paper lanterns. Scoop a goldfish, buy a candy apple, win the ring toss, pick a mask and light a lantern, and when all five are done the fireworks go up over the sea. There is no score, no timer and no way to fail.

    The town is busy around you: groups of festival-goers stop to chat, stall keepers call out, a dog follows you for a while. Morning, day, sunset and night are a click away. It needs a WebGPU browser (current Chrome or Edge on a computer), a keyboard and mouse, and about 11 MB on first load. By the maker of Valley Ride.
