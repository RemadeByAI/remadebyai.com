    About Podium

    Podium turns your phone into a pocket music player with a wheel: turn it to move through menus, press the centre to choose, and press Menu to go back, with a click under your thumb on every step. It plays your local library by album and artist, flips through albums in Cover Flow, shows time-synced lyrics word by word, and keeps playing in the background with lock-screen and Bluetooth controls. It can also search and stream music online through an unofficial YouTube Music client (NewPipeExtractor).

    Much of the fun is in making it yours: six body finishes or any colour, display themes, 17 typefaces, glitter in the case, and stickers cut out of your own photos. There are no ads and no analytics. It needs Android 10 or later and is installed as an APK from GitHub, not from Google Play.
