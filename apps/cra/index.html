    About Cra

    Cra keeps the Arc way of browsing: spaces with their own pinned tabs and favorites, Today tabs that archive themselves, folders, a command bar that searches everything, split view, Little Windows, Peek, Boosts, Easels and Arc's keyboard shortcuts. On first launch it imports Arc's spaces, pinned tabs, favorites, archive and passwords, and it can also bring over tabs, bookmarks and passwords from Chrome, Dia, Zen, Firefox, Safari and others.

    The app is written in Swift on Chrome's own engine, so sites and Chrome extensions work as they do in Chrome, and it has a built-in ad and tracker blocker. It needs macOS 14 or later and runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs; there is also a free iPhone and iPad app (iOS 17) in the style of Arc Search. No account is needed.

    What people say

    Cra is only a few weeks old, so coverage is thin, but what there is comes from Arc users looking for a way out before Arc shuts down. Chris Coyier tried several Arc replacements and called Cra the closest copy, praising the importer and its Little Arc. An AlternativeTo reviewer liked that spaces, pinned tabs and split panes carry over. The complaints so far are early bugs: an extension that could not be removed, and a report on Hacker News that developer tools showed a blank page.

    People like
