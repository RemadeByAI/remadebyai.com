    dumbtv

    Alternative to the Fire TV home screen

    Takes the ads, tracking and clutter out of a Fire TV over ADB with no root, and adds its own clean home screen. Your apps and HDMI inputs stay, and every step can be undone.

    About dumbtv

    It started when the maker's Fire TV Edition set was down to 129 MB of free memory, mostly taken by apps that started on their own and Amazon services that report what you watch. dumbtv turns those off over ADB from a computer, without rooting, and installs an open-source launcher that shows the time, your apps and your HDMI inputs.

    Owners of about twenty other Fire TVs and sticks have run it and reported back. Each change is recorded so it can be undone.

    How it was built with AI

    The maker says they asked a coding agent to clean up their TV over ADB and published the result. Checked 2026-11-22: 78 of 436 commits are co-authored by Claude. TechRadar and XDA covered it as part of people vibe-coding their smart TVs.
