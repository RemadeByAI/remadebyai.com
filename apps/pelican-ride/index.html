    About Pelican Ride

    Catch fish to build combos, fill the frenzy bar for a fish rain and double points, and lose a heart on each crash. Fish you catch buy hats, glasses, scarves, paint, trails and boosts in the garage. There are three modes: adventure, a daily challenge on a fixed track, and a relaxed ride that can't be lost, with time and weather settings and an autopilot.

    Models, terrain, sky, waves, sound and music are all generated in code. It works with touch buttons and gamepads too, in Chinese and English.
