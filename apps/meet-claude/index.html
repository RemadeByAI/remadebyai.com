    About Meet Claude

    A short visual-novel-style story about growing dependence on an AI assistant. Released in March 2026, it has Positive reviews on Steam (96% of 27). The main character shares its name with Anthropic's Claude; the game is an independent satire.

    How it was built with AI

    Its Steam AI disclosure says the writing, code, art, music and deployment were all made with generative AI tools, primarily Claude by Anthropic.

    What people say

    Steam players are broadly positive about Meet Claude. They describe it as a short, thought-provoking satire whose design makes you feel your growing reliance on the AI helper, and many recommend playing it once. Criticism centres on tiring side quests, confusing word puzzles, a story some found predictable, and its heavy use of generative AI. All of the coverage comes from Steam user reviews and a game aggregator. We found no press reviews.

    People like

    • Thought-provoking take on how dependent people become on AI
    • Players say the story is the strongest part
    • The design makes its point by putting the player through it

    Common complaints

    • Side quests and time-pressured tasks get tiring
    • Some found the word games confusing and the ending dragged
    • A few called the story predictable
    • Some players object to its use of generative AI
