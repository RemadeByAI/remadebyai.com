    About Meet Claude

    A short visual-novel-style story about growing dependence on an AI assistant. Released in March 2026, it has Positive reviews on Steam (96% of 27). The main character shares its name with Anthropic's Claude; the game is an independent satire.

    How it was built with AI

    Its Steam AI disclosure says the writing, code, art, music and deployment were all made with generative AI tools, primarily Claude by Anthropic.
