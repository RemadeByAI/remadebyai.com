    About Tab Out

    Tab Out shows your open tabs on a clean grid, grouped by domain, with homepages such as Gmail, YouTube and GitHub pulled into their own group. It flags duplicate tabs, lets you click a tab to jump straight to it in any window, and has a save-for-later list. Closing a group plays a swoosh with confetti.

    Everything stays on your computer: no server, no account and no outside calls. You install it in Chrome as an unpacked extension (Developer mode, Load unpacked), or ask a coding agent to set it up for you.
