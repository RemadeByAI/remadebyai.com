    About Tellyscope

    Tellyscope connects to the TV over your Wi-Fi with ADB, the same debugging link developers use. Its debloat tool matches your TV to one of more than 330 tested lists and shows every app it will turn off before it does; built-in apps are only disabled, never uninstalled, and every change can be undone. It also installs APKs and split APK bundles from your phone, sends files to and from the TV, can set a launcher such as Projectivy as the home screen, and turns on ad-blocking Private DNS.

    The phone doubles as a remote with a D-pad, typing and shortcuts to streaming apps, and an advanced monitor shows CPU, memory and network use per app. You need an Android phone on Android 8.0 or newer on the same Wi-Fi, and developer options turned on on the TV. Get the APK from GitHub, Obtainium or Orion Store; a Google Play version is in testing.

    What people say

    Coverage is small but positive: a how-to article on DigitBin and two YouTube walkthroughs from TV and streaming channels, all from the app's first weeks. They like that it cleans up a Google TV or Fire TV from a phone with no PC or root, and that changes can be undone. DigitBin is careful to say it won't make a cheap TV fast and recommends staying off the alpha builds. GitHub issues show some TVs and Fire TV sticks failing to connect.

    People like

    • Debloats a TV from the phone, with no PC, root or commands
    • Apps are switched off rather than deleted, with an undo
    • Also installs a better launcher and blocks ads

    Common complaints

    • Won't turn a slow, cheap TV into a fast one
    • Some TVs and Fire TV sticks fail to connect
    • Many releases are alpha test builds
