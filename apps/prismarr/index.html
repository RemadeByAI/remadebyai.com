    About Prismarr

    Prismarr sits beside Radarr and Sonarr rather than replacing them. It shows their libraries (several instances side by side), a merged release calendar with iCal export, pending Seerr requests, service health, downloads from qBittorrent, Deluge, Transmission, SABnzbd or NZBGet, one search box for movies, series and people, and optional Plex activity from Tautulli.

    It ships as one Docker container with SQLite inside; the first launch asks for a language and an admin account.
