    About Battle Drones

    Pick a map and mutators on the Skirmish tab, or tap Duel a friend online and send the invite link to play phone to phone. The site also has replays of AI models playing each other. Its maker wrote about how it was built on his blog, simonwillison.net.

    How it was built with AI

    Simon Willison describes it as a vibe coded RTS, built mainly with Claude Opus 5.5 (Fable 5 in early versions); most of the README is written by Claude. Checked 2026-11-22: 286 of 346 commits are co-authored by Claude.

    What people say

    Battle Drones got attention mainly as a case study in agent-driven "vibe coding" rather than as a game. Writers found the multiplayer-over-audio idea the most novel part and noted that Willison himself does not oversell it. Coverage is thin: a few tech newsletters and blogs, a Kottke link, and a small Hacker News thread with only a handful of comments, mostly about build cost and the audio link giving a playtester a headache.

    People like

    • Novel multiplayer over audio, unusual for an RTS
    • Seen as a strong example of what one person can build with coding agents
    • Replay and debug tooling left a process others can inspect

    Common complaints

    • Audio modem sounds were unpleasant enough to cause a headache
    • Acoustic links are sensitive to room noise and speakers
    • Few player reviews; coverage is about the process more than the gameplay
