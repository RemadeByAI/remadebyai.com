    About Rackula

    Drag devices into a rack from a hardware library that uses real front and rear images from the NetBox device-type library. Export layouts to PNG, PDF or SVG for runbooks and change requests, or share them by link or QR code. It handles bayed racks for AV installs and works on a phone in the field.

    Use it at racku.la, or host it yourself with Docker, a Proxmox container or on bare metal, with optional saved layouts and sign-in through local accounts or OIDC.

    What people say

    Write-ups from homelab and tech sites describe Rackula as a quick, intuitive and free way to plan a rack visually, with realistic device images, front and back views, and easy sharing by URL. Reviewers like that it takes less setup and thought than building rack diagrams by hand in general diagram tools. The main caveats are that it is a visualization tool rather than full data-center management software, that its login system was still unfinished for shared deployments, and that the device library has gaps. Coverage is mostly short articles and one editorial review rather than long-term user reports.

    People like

    • Free, open source and quick to start using
    • Intuitive drag-and-drop planning with realistic device images and front and back views
    • Easy export and sharing, including by URL
    • Less effort than drawing racks by hand in general diagram tools

    Common complaints

    • Only a visualization tool, not full data-center management software
    • Login system was unfinished for shared team deployments
    • Device library has gaps for some homelab hardware, and there is no mobile support
