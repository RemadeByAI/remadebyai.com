    About GiroCrab

    GiroCrab is a native SwiftUI app on a Rust core. It connects directly to banks (FinTS/HBCI, PSD2 and many dedicated connectors), sorts transactions into a category tree with rules and merchant recognition, shows savings plans and loan repayments, and sends transfers including instant SEPA payments. Data stays in an AES-256 encrypted SQLCipher vault on your device.

    The Mac app is the main version, there are Linux packages, and the iPhone and iPad app is in TestFlight beta. The project is about two weeks old and moving fast.

    What people say

    German Mac press and forum users are mostly positive. They like that it is free, keeps data locally and imports from MoneyMoney smoothly, and forum testers say development moves fast, with reported security issues fixed within hours. Caveats include an early development stage, banks or security procedures that do not work yet, and some readers questioning how much of the project is AI-generated.

    People like

    • Free, local and open source, with no subscription or tracking
    • Built-in migration assistant; the MoneyMoney import works well
    • Very responsive developers who fix reported issues quickly

    Common complaints

    • Early stage: users should check their bank is supported first
    • Some banks and older HBCI security procedures cannot be set up yet
    • Some readers suspect it is largely vibe-coded
