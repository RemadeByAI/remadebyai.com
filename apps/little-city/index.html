    About Little City

    There are no rules to follow: place blocks on the water and they grow into houses, harbours, castles and lighthouses, all drawn in code with no art files. Then fly down into the streets and watch the town go about its day, from market squares to weddings.

    Over 80 special buildings and events are hidden in it (a guide on the site lists them), "grow a town" builds a whole one for you, and yes, you can nuke it: they'll build it back. Free, and it works on phones too.
