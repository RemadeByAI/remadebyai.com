    About IMAGO

    IMAGO opens the photos on your own Immich server (version 2.6.0 or later, connected with an API key) or in your phone's gallery and edits them without touching the original: exposure, contrast, highlights and shadows, colour, a tone curve, an eight-band colour mixer, colour grading wheels, linear and radial masks, crop and straighten. Every edit is kept as a recipe you can change later, and exports go back to Immich at full resolution, stacked on the original.

    It needs Android 12 or later and runs its adjustments on the device's GPU at 16 bits per channel. A composer turns photos into stories, posts and carousels. An optional account syncs recipes and presets between devices, but photos never sync and there are no ads or analytics. A Windows desktop app is in the same repo, but it has to be built from source; the site only links to Google Play. An optional supporter plan costs EUR 1 a month.
