    About Terminactory

    You place drills, furnaces and other machines, link them with belts, power them with solar panels and later steam, and sell what they make at a trade post. Research unlocks better machines on the way to computers and oil, and nothing attacks you or breaks at random. An in-game browser holds a wiki, production stats, the research tree and a small fake web of forums and sites full of optional lore.

    It is meant to sit next to your coding agents: a Claude Code hook (or a tfac msg command for other tools) shows the agent's replies in the game as telegrams. You build it from source with Rust 1.88 or newer and need a UTF-8 terminal; it works on macOS and Linux, and on Windows through WSL.
