    About SoloMD

    Write in pure WYSIWYG, live preview or split view, with focus and typewriter modes, Vim mode, math and Mermaid diagrams. Notes stay as .md files in a plain folder, with wikilinks and backlinks, and each folder can sync encrypted through iCloud Drive, OneDrive, WebDAV, S3 or Git. One export window writes PDF, Word, HTML, EPUB, LaTeX, ODT, RTF and plain text.

    It is a small app (about 14 MB on Windows), free and MIT-licensed, with no account or subscription. Optional AI features work with local models such as Ollama or your own API keys.
