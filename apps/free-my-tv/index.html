    About Free My TV

    Free My TV replaces the TV's home screen with a glass shelf of the streaming apps you use, with no sponsored rows or autoplaying promos. Every hour it picks a few trending titles, only from apps you already have, and it can show Continue Watching, the weather, live crypto prices, your HDMI inputs and what's playing. It switches between light and dark at your city's sunset.

    On Google TV and Android TV you install it with adb from the ready APK, or ask a coding assistant (Claude Code, Codex or Antigravity) to build and install it for you. LG and Samsung TVs get their own builds. Nothing is uninstalled and there is no root.

    How it was built with AI

    The README says "Built with Claude Code" and the app is made to be adapted by AI through its CLAUDE.md. Checked 2026-11-22 in the repo's commit history: 579 of 803 commits are co-authored by Claude.

    What people say

    Coverage is limited to a couple of blog write-ups that followed a wider October 2026 news wave about people using AI to strip ads from smart TVs. Writers liked that it needs no rooted TV or separate computer and that it ships with instructions so an AI assistant can adapt it to other TVs. One guide was openly cautious: the app needs powerful permissions, TV makers can break it with updates, and anyone adapting it with AI should review the generated code.

    People like

    • Works through the TV's own debugging interface, with no root or PC needed
    • Designed so an AI coding assistant can adapt it to other TV models
    • Openly documents its own risks

    Common complaints

    • Needs powerful permissions, including accessibility access and the ability to disable other apps
    • TV system updates may break it
    • Young project with open questions about long-term maintenance
