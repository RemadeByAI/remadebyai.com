    About Prism

    Prism shows Google, iCloud and iCal calendars on a screen you already own, with chores and reminders whose points add up to a reward a kid picked, shared shopping lists with a barcode scanner, recipes, and your photos when the house is quiet.

    It runs on a Raspberry Pi 4 or newer, in Docker, or as a Home Assistant add-on, with PIN sign-in and no accounts. It is free for non-commercial use.
