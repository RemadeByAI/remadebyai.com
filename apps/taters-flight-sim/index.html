    About Tater's Flight Sim

    Fly a Cessna 172RG, an F-16C, an A-10C or a Boeing 747-400 over a big world of farmland, forests, rivers, towns, a coastline and snow-capped mountains. The flight physics are real, so you can stall, spin and crash. The jets carry guns and missiles for tank busting and dogfights with enemy jets and helicopters, and each aircraft has a working cockpit with live displays.

    Every model, texture and sound is generated in code. There is no hosted version yet: you run it yourself with Node.js and play in a browser with WebGL2 (a GPU is needed).
