    About EffectCraft

    EffectCraft is a compositor with compositions, layers, keyframes, a graph editor, more than 300 effects, layer styles, expressions, 3D cameras and lights, and a render queue that exports ProRes, HEVC, AV1, WebM, image sequences and GIF.

    Its README says macOS is the most tested platform and that Linux and Windows users have hit basic interaction bugs. The project calls itself alpha.

    How it was built with AI

    Checked 2026-11-22 in the repo's commit history: 1,660 of 5,432 commits are co-authored by Claude, and the repo has CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md files. XDA (Nov 2026) says it was "built in 10 days, mostly by AI agents" using Claude Opus 5.5.

    What people say

    Coverage is thin: two blog write-ups and no community discussion of the app itself. An Italian tech blogger tested the command-line renderer and found that rendering, expressions and Lottie export mostly worked. He calls it the most ambitious app in the suite and a real option on Linux, but not for anyone who has to deliver complex After Effects projects. A second review praises the large effects catalogue and the automation features, but says it is not yet an After Effects replacement.

    People like

    • Very ambitious feature set with hundreds of effects
    • Command-line rendering and Lottie export work in testing
    • A real option for motion graphics on Linux

    Common complaints

    • Cannot open After Effects .aep projects or use Adobe plug-ins
    • Still alpha and not ready to replace After Effects
    • Some effects lost on Lottie export
