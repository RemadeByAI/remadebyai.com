    About Cayley

    The flight model builds each aircraft from its published geometry, masses, engine and propeller: the wing is a lifting line of 40 strips, the tail flies in the wing's actual wake and the propeller is modelled blade by blade, so stalls, induced drag and roll damping come out of the physics rather than a lookup table. Each of the nine aircraft was flown headlessly against its own handbook, with 213 of 223 checks inside tolerance and the misses published.

    It has a working cockpit, synthesised sound and live weather over real terrain anywhere on Earth. It needs WebGL 2 in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox; a keyboard is enough, and a joystick or gamepad is picked up automatically.
