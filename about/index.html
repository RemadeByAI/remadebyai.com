    ✦ About

    Everyday tools, rebuilt around how people work.

    RemadeByAI is a directory from EmuKey LLC. It collects practical software that takes a familiar tool and rebuilds it with AI as part of how it gets made.

    What you'll find
    EmuKey's own apps, like InCloseMail and DesktopWiki, alongside open-source projects worth a look. Each app has its own page with what it replaces, where it runs, its licence and how AI helped build it.
    How apps are chosen
    Each listing is a working alternative to software you probably already use. Open-source picks are made by their own developers, not by EmuKey.
