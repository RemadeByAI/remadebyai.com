    ✦ Alternatives

    AI-built alternatives to Wispr Flow, Otter.ai and Granola

    Dictation, transcription and meeting-notes apps. Several run speech recognition on your own computer, so your recordings stay private. 6 apps. More alternatives

    🎙Open source

    FreeFlow

    Alternative to Wispr Flow

    Free voice dictation for your computer. Speak and it types for you, with no monthly subscription.

    ProductivityDetails →
    👄Open source

    Lipflow

    Alternative to Wispr Flow (silent)

    Dictation without sound: hold a key, silently mouth your words, and the text appears at your cursor. Reads your lips through the webcam, locally.

    ProductivityDetails →
    ◉Open source

    Mila

    Alternative to MacWhisper

    A Mac app that records meetings, dictation and voice memos and transcribes them on the device, in English or Hebrew, with speaker labels and optional summaries.

    AudioDetails →
    ◔Open source

    Minute

    Alternative to Granola, Otter.ai

    A Mac meeting note-taker that works with no internet at all. It notices when a call starts, transcribes it live, then writes a summary, decisions and action items with a local model.

    ProductivityDetails →
    ◎Open source

    Parrot

    Alternative to Otter.ai, Granola

    A Mac meeting recorder that transcribes any call on your own computer, with no bot joining. An optional assistant suggests answers from your documents during the call.

    ProductivityDetails →
    ◉Open source

    Voicebox

    Alternative to ElevenLabs and Wispr Flow

    A local AI voice studio: clone a voice from a few seconds of audio, generate speech in 23 languages, and dictate into any app with a hotkey. Everything runs on your own computer.
