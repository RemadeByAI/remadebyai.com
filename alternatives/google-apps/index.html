    AI-built alternatives to Google apps

    Alternatives to Google Docs, Calendar, Contacts, Workspace, Lens and the Google TV home screen, for people who want to rely less on Google. 9 apps. More alternatives

    Calino

    Alternative to Google Calendar

    A calendar in your browser that connects to your own CalDAV or JMAP server, such as Nextcloud, Fastmail or Radicale. It handles events, tasks and journal notes, with no Calino account.

    ▣Open source

    Clean Your TV

    Alternative to the stock Google TV home screen

    Strips the ads, tracking and preloaded bloat from an Android TV or Google TV over Wi-Fi and swaps in a clean, Apple TV-style home screen. No root, nothing uninstalled, and one command undoes it all.

    TVDetails →
    ▶Open source

    Free My TV

    Alternative to the stock Google TV, LG and Samsung home screens

    A fast, ad-free home screen for Google TV, Android TV, LG webOS and Samsung Tizen TVs, with your own apps up front, trending picks from them, weather and a screensaver. One button brings the factory home screen back.

    LibrePaper

    Alternative to Overleaf and Google Docs comments

    Write and review papers together in the browser: LaTeX, Typst, Markdown, Quarto, HTML or Org, with live highlights, comments and suggested edits.

    Mailflare

    Alternative to Google Workspace

    Self-hosted email on your own domain, plus calendar, booking links and file storage, that runs on Cloudflare or in one Docker container. Receiving and sending can be free.

    Natel

    Alternative to Google Contacts

    A small web app for editing the contacts on your own CardDAV server (Baïkal, Radicale, Nextcloud and others) from any browser.

    PlayTranslate

    Alternative to Google Lens screen translation

    Translates the text on your Android screen in games, visual novels and manga with one tap, or automatically as dialogue changes. Works offline and helps you learn with word lookup and Anki cards.

    Alternative to Google Inactive Account Manager

    An iPhone app for writing sealed letters and passwords that open for the people you choose if you stop checking in. Everything is encrypted on your phone.
